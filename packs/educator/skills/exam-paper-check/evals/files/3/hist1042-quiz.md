## Section A - Answer all questions

**Q1.**
(a) Define the term 'proto-industrialisation'.  [2 marks]
(b) Name two regions of Britain where proto-industrial textile production was significant before 1780.  [2 marks]
(Total: 4 marks)

**Q2.**
(a) Describe two features of parliamentary enclosure between 1760 and 1820.  [3 marks]
(b) Explain why the Corn Laws were a disaster for everyone in Britain.  [3 marks]
(Total: 6 marks)

**Q3.** Read Source A (an extract from a Manchester mill owner's evidence to the 1832 Sadler Committee) and Source B (a factory inspector's report, 1836).
(a) What does Source A suggest about working hours for children?  [3 marks]
(b) How far does Source B support Source A?  [3 marks]
(c) How useful are Sources A and B together for studying factory reform? Explain your answer.  [4 marks]
(Total: 10 marks)

## Section B - Answer EITHER Question 4 OR Question 5

**OR**

**Q5.** 'Free trade, not industry, made Britain rich in the mid-nineteenth century.'

(Total: 12 marks)
