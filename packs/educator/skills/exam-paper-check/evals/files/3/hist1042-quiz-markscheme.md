# HIST1042 Week 8 Quiz - Mark Scheme (for markers and moderator)

General: credit any valid point supported by accurate evidence. Use levels for extended answers.

## Section A

**Q1(a)** [2] - 1 mark for rural/household-based manufacture; 1 mark for production for distant markets before the factory system.

**Q1(b)** [2] - 1 mark each, e.g. West Riding of Yorkshire, Lancashire, East Anglia (worsted), the Midlands (hosiery).

**Q2(a)** [3] - 1 mark per feature described, up to 3: Acts of Parliament per parish; consolidation of strips; loss of common rights; hedging and fencing costs.

**Q2(b)** [3] - Up to 3 marks for explanation: high bread prices; benefit to landowners; Anti-Corn Law League agitation; repeal 1846.

**Q3(a)** [3] - 1 mark per valid inference from Source A, supported by a quotation.

**Q3(b)** [3] - 1 mark for agreement, 1 for disagreement, 1 for an overall judgement.

**Q3(c)** [3] - Level 1 (1): comments on one source. Level 2 (2): both sources, provenance considered. Level 3 (3): combined utility judged with reference to purpose.

## Section B

**Q4(a)** [5] - 1 mark per change outlined, up to 5: speed, cost per ton-mile, bulk goods (coal), perishable goods, national markets.

**Q4(b)** [10] - Level 1 (1-3) descriptive; Level 2 (4-6) some argument; Level 3 (7-8) balanced argument; Level 4 (9-10) sustained, well-evidenced judgement weighing other causes (coal, cotton, overseas trade).

**Q5(a)** [4] - 1 mark per step, up to 4: Huskisson's tariff reforms of the 1820s; Peel's budgets of 1842 and 1845; repeal of the Corn Laws 1846; repeal of the Navigation Acts 1849; Cobden-Chevalier Treaty 1860.

**Q5(b)** [8] - Level 1 (1-2); Level 2 (3-4); Level 3 (5-6); Level 4 (7-8) sustained judgement.

## Section C

**Q7(a)** [5] - 1 mark per claim identified (max 2); 1 mark per supported/unsupported judgement using Source D (max 2); 1 mark for a precise reference to the census figures.

**Q7(b)** [10] - Level 1 (1-3) content only; Level 2 (4-6) provenance; Level 3 (7-8) provenance and context; Level 4 (9-10) sustained evaluation using both sources and own knowledge.

Total for quiz: 50 marks.
