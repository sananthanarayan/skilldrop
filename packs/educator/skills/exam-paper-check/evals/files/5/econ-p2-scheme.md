# Calderbrook Sixth Form
## Year 13 Economics Mock - Paper 2 Mark Scheme

**Marking notes**
- Levels-based questions: decide the level first, then the mark within the level.
- Credit relevant use of the extracts and of real-world examples.
- Calculations: accept answers that follow from a correct method.
- Do not credit diagrams that are unlabelled.

### Section A

| Question | Guidance | Marks |
|---|---|---|
| 1(a) | Unemployment caused by a lack of aggregate demand in a downturn (1); linked to the economic cycle / recession (1) | 2 |
| 1(b) | Correct method: (new - old) / old x 100 (1); correct answer to 1 d.p. (1) | 2 |
| 1(c) | Identifies inflation falling from 7.8% to 3.2% and unemployment rising from 3.7% to 5.1% (1); explains a link, e.g. tighter policy reduced AD, lowering inflation and raising unemployment, or Phillips curve trade-off (up to 3) | 4 |

**Total for Section A: 40 marks**
