# Hollinsford Academy
## Year 10 Mathematics Mock Examination
### Paper 1 (Non-calculator) - Foundation/Higher crossover

Time allowed: 1 hour 15 minutes
You must NOT use a calculator.
Total for paper: 48 marks

---

**Question 1**
(a) Simplify fully 3x + 5y - x + 2y  **[1]**
(b) Expand and simplify (x + 3)(x - 2)  **[2]**
*(Total for Question 1: 3 marks)*

**Question 2**
(a) Write 0.375 as a fraction in its simplest form.  **[2]**
(b) Work out 2 3/4 divided by 1 1/2. Give your answer as a mixed number.  **[2]**
*(Total for Question 2: 4 marks)*

**Question 3**

A bag contains only red, blue and green counters in the ratio 3 : 4 : 5. There are 60 counters in the bag.
(a) How many blue counters are in the bag?  **[2]**
(b) Six red counters are removed. A counter is then taken at random. Work out the probability that it is green.  **[3]**
(c) Write the new ratio red : blue : green in its simplest form.  **[2]**
*(Total for Question 3: 8 marks)*

**Question 4**

The diagram (not drawn to scale) shows triangle ABC with AB = AC and angle BAC = 40 degrees.
(a) Work out the size of angle ABC.  **[3]**
(b) Give a reason for each step of your working in part (a).  **[2]**
(d) A different triangle, PQR, has base 8 cm and perpendicular height 6 cm. Work out its area.  **[3]**
*(Total for Question 4: 8 marks)*

**Question 5**
(a) Solve 4x - 7 = 2x + 9  **[2]**
(b) Solve 3(x - 2) = 12  **[2]**
*(Total for Question 5: 4 marks)*

**Question 6**

A straight line passes through the points (1, 3) and (4, 12).
(a) Work out the gradient of the line.  **[2]**
(b) Find the equation of the line.  **[3]**
(c) Does the point (10, 30) lie on the line? Show how you decide.  **[3]**
*(Total for Question 6: 8 marks)*

**Question 7**
(a) Estimate the value of (48.7 x 3.1) / 0.49  **[2]**
(b) Work out the square root of 37, correct to 2 decimal places.  **[3]**
*(Total for Question 7: 5 marks)*

**Question 8**
(a) Prove that the sum of any three consecutive integers is a multiple of 3.  **[4]**
(b) Prove that the product of two odd numbers is always odd.  **[4]**
*(Total for Question 8: 8 marks)*

**END OF PAPER**
