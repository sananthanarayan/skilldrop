# Year 9 Science — End of Unit Test: Forces and Energy

Time allowed: 50 minutes
Total marks: 50

Answer ALL questions in Section A. In Section B, answer either Question 6 or Question 7.

## Section A

**1** A trolley is pushed along a bench.

(a) Name the force that slows the trolley down. [1]

(b) The trolley has a mass of 2 kg and accelerates at 3 m/s². Calculate the resultant force on it. Show your working. [3]

(c) State the unit of force. [1]

(Total for Question 1 = 5 marks)

**2** A student drops a ball from a height of 1.8 m.

(a) (i) Describe the energy transfer as the ball falls. [2]

(ii) Calculate the gravitational potential energy store of the 0.5 kg ball at the start. g = 10 N/kg. [3]

(iii) Explain why the ball does not bounce back to 1.8 m. [2]

(b) Suggest one way to reduce errors when measuring the height. [1]

(Total for Question 2 = 8 marks)

**3** Electric kettles transfer energy by heating.

(a) Define efficiency. [1]

(c) A kettle is 80% efficient. 2400 J is supplied. Calculate the useful energy transferred. [3]

(d) Suggest why the kettle is not 100% efficient. [2]

(Total for Question 3 = 6 marks)

**4** Describe how you would investigate how the extension of a spring depends on the force applied. Include a risk assessment. [6]

(Total for Question 4 = 6 marks)

**5** A car travels 300 m in 20 s.

(a) Calculate its average speed. [2]

(b) Sketch a distance–time graph for a car moving at constant speed. [2]

(Total for Question 5 = 4 marks)
