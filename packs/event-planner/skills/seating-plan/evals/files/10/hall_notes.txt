Bramblecote Village Hall - main hall, notes for hirers

Tables: 10 numbered tables. Tables 1-9 are round and seat 8. Table 10 is the small
rectangular table next to the quizmaster's desk and seats 6.

Layout: the stage is at the north end. Tables 1-6 are on the main floor, in two rows of
three - 1, 2, 3 nearest the entrance doors (south), 4, 5, 6 behind them. Tables 7-10 are
on the raised area in front of the stage, up two steps, with no ramp. Table 10 is at the
east end of the raised area. The bar is on the east wall; toilets (including the
accessible WC) are off the entrance lobby.

The hall team set up the tables to your plan on the afternoon of the event - please give
them a list of table numbers and how many chairs at each.
