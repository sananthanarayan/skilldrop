Ashgrove Academy Year 13 Leavers' Prom - Fri 2 July 2027 - Brackenridge Hall (main hall)

13 round tables, 8 chairs each - no extra chairs (the venue was firm about this, fire exits).
Tables 1 and 2 are on the level by the ramp from the car park entrance. Tables 3-13 are down the
three steps on the main floor, so anyone in a wheelchair has to be on table 1 or 2 (max 2 wheelchairs a table).
Table 13 is the staff table by the fire doors - all six staff sit there.

Layout from the stage end:

Everyone in a friendship group (from the ticket form) sits together - that's what they were promised.
