# Ashby Lane Community Orchard — Autumn Update

In today's fast-paced world, community spaces matter more than ever. This season 37 volunteers planted 112 new fruit trees along the east boundary, taking the orchard to just over 300 trees.

Special thanks to Олена Коваль, who led the Saturday grafting sessions and taught more than twenty of us to graft our own rootstock.

Our grant from the Fernvale Trust (£4,250) paid for new tools and rootstock. Figures are provisional until the annual audit in January.

Join us for the apple har​vest festival on Saturday 8 November, 11am to 3pm, at the orchard gate.
