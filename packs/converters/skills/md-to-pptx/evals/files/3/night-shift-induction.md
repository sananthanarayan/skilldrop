# Night shift induction: Wakefield cross-dock

Ops team, version 3. Read before your first shift.

# Before your first shift

- Collect your badge from security at Gate 2
- Book the forklift refresher if your licence is over 12 months old
  - Counterbalance and reach trucks only
    - Reach trucks: the refresher includes the 9 m racking test
      - Bring your old licence card to the test
- Read the [incident reporting section](#reporting-an-incident) before you start

# Start-of-shift checks

The checks take about 25 minutes. Do them in this order.

1. Sign in on the shift board in the transport office
2. Pick up a handheld scanner from the charging rack
3. Log in to the scanner with your badge number
4. Check the scanner battery is above 80%
5. Walk the dock doors 1 to 14 and check each leveller is down
6. Check the fire exits on the east wall are clear
7. Read the handover notes from the late shift
9. Check the trailer schedule for the first three arrivals
10. Confirm the yard shunter has the same schedule
11. Test the dock door 7 sensor (it fails most often)
12. Check the chilled bay reads 2 to 5 °C
13. Report any fault to the shift lead before 22:30

If the scanner fails at check 3, go back to step 9 and tell the shift lead.

# Reporting an incident

Report every incident within the shift, even with no injury.[^1]

<details>
<summary>Old paper form</summary>
Form OPS-7 is retired; do not use it.
</details>

The scanner command to open an incident is:

```text
MENU > 7 > INCIDENT > NEW
```

[^1]: Near misses count as incidents.
