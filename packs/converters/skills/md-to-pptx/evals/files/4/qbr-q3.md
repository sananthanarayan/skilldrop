# Harlow Freight: Q3 2026 Business Review

Prepared by Northwind Logistics Services for Harlow Freight, 22 October 2026

## Summary

- On-time delivery for the quarter was **94.2%** against a 95% target
- Shipments handled: 18,430 (up 6% on Q2)
- Damage claims fell from 41 to 27
- Two lanes are well below target and have recovery plans (see Risks)

## Risks and mitigations

1. **Toledo to Milwaukee on-time at 91.7%**
   - Owner: Priya Raman (Northwind)
   - Cause: driver shortage on the Thursday wave
   - Mitigation: second carrier contracted from 1 November
2. **Cincinnati to Memphis dock congestion**
   - Owner: Owen Castell (Harlow)
   - Cause: shared dock doors with inbound returns
   - Mitigation: move returns to doors 9 to 12 after 2 pm
3. **Dayton to Nashville weather exposure in Q4**
   - Owner: Priya Raman (Northwind)
   - Mitigation: pre-agreed reroute through Louisville
4. **Peak season volume (+22% forecast for November)**
   - Owner: Jonah Wilde (Northwind)
   - Mitigation: 14 additional trailers on short-term lease
   - Mitigation: weekend dispatch cover from 8 November
5. **Fuel surcharge volatility**
   - Owner: Mei Takahashi (Harlow)
   - Mitigation: monthly surcharge review instead of quarterly
6. **Telematics vendor contract ends 31 December**
   - Owner: Jonah Wilde (Northwind)
   - Mitigation: renewal or replacement decision by 15 November
7. **Akron to Charlotte just below target (94.6%)**
   - Owner: Priya Raman (Northwind)
   - Mitigation: earlier cut-off for Friday pickups
8. **Proof-of-delivery photo gaps**
   - Owner: Owen Castell (Harlow)
   - Cause: older handheld scanners at two depots
   - Mitigation: scanner swap at Dayton and Akron in November
9. **Claims process takes 19 days on average**
   - Owner: Mei Takahashi (Harlow)
   - Mitigation: target 10 days with a shared claims inbox
10. **Single point of contact for after-hours escalations**
    - Owner: Jonah Wilde (Northwind)
    - Mitigation: rota of three named duty managers from December
11. **Yard capacity at Columbus during peak**
    - Owner: Owen Castell (Harlow)
    - Mitigation: overflow yard at Grove Road, 40 spaces
12. **Data feed delays into Harlow's planning system**
    - Owner: Priya Raman (Northwind)
    - Cause: nightly batch finishes after 6 am
    - Mitigation: move batch start to 1 am
