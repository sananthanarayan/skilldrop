# Grounds Maintenance Tender: Response from Wexcombe Grounds Ltd

Prepared for Thornbury Parish Council, tender reference GM-2027.

## 1. About us

Wexcombe Grounds Ltd has maintained parks, churchyards and verges across the Wexcombe valley since 2011. We employ 14 full-time staff and hold all licences required for herbicide use near water.

![Our team at Elmfield Rec](img/team-photo.png)

## 2. Our approach

### 2.1 Grass cutting

- 24 cuts a year on the recreation ground and churchyard
  - Fortnightly April to September
  - Monthly in March and October
- Verge cuts timed to avoid the wildflower strip on Mill Road

### 2.2 Hedges and trees

1. Hedge cutting outside the bird-nesting season (March to August)
2. Annual tree safety walk with a written report
3. Emergency call-out within 4 hours for fallen limbs
