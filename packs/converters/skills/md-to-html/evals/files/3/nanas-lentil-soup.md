# Nana's lentil soup

serves 4 (or 2 v hungry people)

## you need
- 1 onion
- 2 carrots
- 200g red lentils
- 1.2 litres veg stock
- 1 tsp cumin
- half a lemon

## what to do
1. chop onion and carrots, soften in a bit of oil 10 mins
2. add cumin, stir 1 min
2. add lentils & stock, simmer 25 mins
4. blend half of it, squeeze in the lemon
