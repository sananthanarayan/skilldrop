# Waterfold Water — Customer Assistance Policy, section 4: Hardship Payment Plan

Version 3.0. Effective 1 September 2026. Supersedes version 2.2 in full.

4.1 Eligibility. A household is eligible if its gross annual household income is below £21,000, OR if any member of the household receives a qualifying means-tested benefit (Schedule B).

4.2 Term. Arrears are repaid in equal monthly instalments over up to 24 months, on top of ongoing charges.

4.3 Evidence. The applicant provides EITHER the last 2 months of bank statements for the household's main account, OR proof of a qualifying benefit. Not both. A copy of a bill is not required; we hold the account details.

4.4 Decision. A decision is issued within 10 working days of receiving the evidence.

4.5 Collection activity. All collection activity on the account (reminder letters, referral to debt collection agents) is paused from the day an application is received until the decision is issued.

4.6 Cancellation. A plan is cancelled if two consecutive monthly payments are missed. Before cancelling, we write to the customer and give 14 days to make the missed payment.

4.7 Reapplication. A customer whose plan is cancelled may reapply after 6 months.
