Customer Service notes:
- Several customers say the booking call came from a Brightlane number, not from Oakhollow. Brightlane confirms they "sometimes call to book when the warehouse hasn't got round to it". The Operations Manager's notes say the warehouse makes all booking calls.
- For damaged items, customers report being told by Customer Service to "wait for the warehouse to call" and by the warehouse to "call Customer Service".
- Customer Service cannot see proof of delivery without emailing Brightlane, which takes 1–2 days to answer.
