# Compliance note: telemetry records

Prepared by: Glenmarra Water Compliance team

The drinking-water regulator requires us to keep continuous records of treated-water flow and chlorine residual at every pumping station and service reservoir.

- Records must be retained for at least two years.
- Any gap of more than 15 minutes in flow or chlorine residual readings at a site must be reported to the regulator as a data-loss incident within 5 working days.
- Repeated data-loss incidents at the same site can lead to an enforcement notice.

Readings that are captured at the site and back-filled after a communications outage count as continuous, provided their original timestamps are preserved.
