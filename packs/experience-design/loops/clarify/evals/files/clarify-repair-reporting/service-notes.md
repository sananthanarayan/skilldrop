# How repairs work behind the form

*Notes from the repairs team lead, Ollerby Housing Trust (fictional)*

## Priorities and timescales

- **Emergency**: attended within 24 hours. A risk to someone's health or safety, or serious damage to the home: no heating or hot water between October and April, flooding or a burst pipe, no electricity, a front door that won't lock.
- **Urgent**: completed within 5 working days.
- **Routine**: completed within 20 working days.

## What happens after someone submits

1. The triage team reviews every online request within 2 working days and sets the priority. They change it if the tenant picked the wrong one. About a third of requests marked E are changed to U or R.
2. Triage passes the job to one of our contractors.
3. The contractor's booking system texts the tenant an appointment slot. It can only do this if we hold a mobile number on the tenancy record. About 30% of tenancy records have no mobile number. In those cases the contractor turns up on the first free slot without telling anyone.
4. Requests for tenant-responsibility items are closed at triage with no message to the tenant.

## Tenant-responsibility items (Schedule 3, short version)

Curtain rails, light bulbs, internal door handles, toilet seats, plugs for sinks and baths.

## Other facts

- The tenancy reference is printed on the top right of every rent statement. Format: OHT- then 6 digits, for example OHT-123456.
- Photos: up to 3, each no bigger than 5 MB.
- The form doesn't ask for a phone number. Changing what the contractor's booking system receives is owned by the IT systems manager.
