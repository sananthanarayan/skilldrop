# Report a repair

*Ollerby Housing Trust (fictional), page at www.ollerby-housing.example/repairs*

Responsive repairs are categorised in accordance with the Trust's Repairs Policy (2019) as Emergency (E), Urgent (U) or Routine (R). Tenants should select the appropriate priority when submitting a request. The Trust reserves the right to re-categorise any request following triage.

Please note that the Trust is not responsible for repairs which are the tenant's responsibility under the tenancy agreement (see Schedule 3). Requests of this nature will be closed without further action.

To submit a request you will need your tenancy reference. You may upload photographs to assist the triage process.

Once your request is received it will be triaged and allocated to an approved contractor who will be in touch.

If you smell gas, do not use this form. Call [gas emergency number].

For out-of-hours emergencies call 01632 960 418.
