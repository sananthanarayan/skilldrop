# Garden Waste Collection Service

*Ashcombe Vale Council: ashcombevale.example/bins/garden-waste*

The Council provides a chargeable garden waste collection service to households within the district, in accordance with the Environmental Protection Act 1990 and the Council's Waste Collection Policy. Participation in the scheme is voluntary and subject to the terms and conditions set out below.

## Subscription

Subscriptions run from 1 March to 30 November inclusive and are payable annually. The annual charge is £52 per bin. Households may subscribe for up to three bins. Subscriptions taken out part-way through the year are charged at the full annual rate. Subscriptions are non-transferable and non-refundable.

## Collections

Collections are made fortnightly on the household's scheduled collection day. Please refer to your collection calendar. Collections are suspended from December to February.

## Bins and permits

New subscribers will be supplied with a brown wheeled bin, subject to availability. Existing bin holders must quote the serial number of their bin when subscribing. A permit sticker will be dispatched and must be affixed to the bin before collections can commence. Bins not displaying a valid permit sticker will not be emptied.

## Acceptable materials

Grass cuttings, hedge trimmings, leaves, flowers, weeds and small branches. Not accepted: soil, rubble, food waste, plastic bags, pet waste, Japanese knotweed.

## Renewal

Subscribers will be invited to renew in February each year. Failure to renew by the stated date will result in the suspension of collections.

## Terms and conditions

By subscribing, the householder agrees to the terms and conditions of the scheme, which may be varied by the Council from time to time.
