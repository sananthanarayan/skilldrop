## What happens behind the form today

- A leisure services officer checks each application by hand: the address on the bill against the address entered, then the eligibility document.
- Rejections go out as the standard rejection email. It doesn't say which document failed or why.
- The address lookup uses the council's address list. Customer services say it is updated "a few times a year".
- No update is sent between the confirmation screen and the card arriving.
