# Leisure Access Card: concessionary rates

*Hollinsby Borough Council (fictional), page at www.hollinsby.example/leisure-card*

The Leisure Access Card entitles eligible residents of the Borough to concessionary rates at Council-operated leisure facilities.

## Eligibility

Applicants must be resident within the Borough and meet one of the following criteria:

- in receipt of a qualifying benefit
- aged 65 or over
- in full-time education

## Supporting documentation

Applicants must upload:

1. Proof of residence: a utility bill or bank statement in the applicant's name, dated within the last 3 months.
2. Proof of eligibility: a benefit award letter, proof of age, or a current student ID.

Applications without satisfactory documentation will be declined.

## Processing

Applications are processed within 15 working days. Cards are posted to the address provided.

Enquiries: leisure@hollinsby.example
