# Resident Parking Permits

*Brindlemoor District Council: brindlemoor.example/parking/resident-permits*

## Introduction

Brindlemoor District Council operates a number of Controlled Parking Zones (CPZs) within the district under the provisions of the Brindlemoor (Controlled Parking Zones) Traffic Management Order 2019, as amended. Within a CPZ, parking in designated resident bays is restricted to vehicles displaying a valid resident parking permit issued for the relevant zone during the hours of operation shown on the zone entry signage.

## Eligibility

Permits may be issued to residents whose principal place of residence is situated within a CPZ and who are the registered keeper of the vehicle, or who can otherwise demonstrate that the vehicle is in their care and control on an ongoing basis. Satisfactory evidence of residency and of the vehicle's details will be required. The Council reserves the right to request further evidence at its discretion.

## Charges

The permit charge is payable annually in advance. The charge for a first permit is £68. The charge for a second permit at the same address is £112. Permits are not transferable between vehicles except on application. Refunds are not normally given.

## Visitor permits

Residents within a CPZ are also entitled to purchase visitor permits, subject to annual limits.

## How to apply

Applications should be made using the online form. Applicants must ensure that all evidence submitted is legible and current. Incomplete applications cannot be processed and will be returned. Permits will be issued in due course following verification of the application.
