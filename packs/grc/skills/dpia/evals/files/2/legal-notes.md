# Notes from the in-house legal team (one-page summary)

- We are the controller for member data; Iriscale is our processor under the draft data processing agreement (DPA). The draft DPA has not been signed.
- The draft DPA is silent on the vendor's use of data for model improvement.
- No transfer mechanism is listed in the draft DPA.
- Membership terms say members "may be refused entry if identity cannot be verified". There is currently no manual fallback at the turnstile for a failed match other than calling reception staff, who are not on site from 22:00 to 06:00 at the 9 clubs that open 24 hours.
