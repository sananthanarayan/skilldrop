# Iriscale Gate — vendor factsheet (supplied by the vendor)

- Templates are mathematical representations, not photos. The turnstile camera image is discarded after matching.
- Enrolment photo is kept to allow re-enrolment if the algorithm is updated.
- All customer data is hosted in data centres within the EU.
- 24/7 technical support is provided by our support centre outside the EEA; support engineers have remote read access to match logs (membership number, club, timestamp, match score) to troubleshoot.
- Templates and enrolment photos are retained for 24 months after a customer's account closes and may be used, in pseudonymised form, to improve Iriscale's matching models.
- Customer may configure the match threshold. Default threshold is tuned for speed.
