# Brackenford Records Ltd — Information Security Policy

Version 2.1 · Approved 14 March 2024 · Review: annually

1. Scope. All staff, systems and facilities, including the Northgate and Riverside storage warehouses and the scanning bureau.
2. Encryption. All scanned client images are encrypted at rest using AES-256. Laptops use full-disk encryption.
3. Access. Multi-factor authentication is required for all remote access to company systems.
4. Incident management. Security incidents affecting client data are reported to the affected client within 72 hours of confirmation.
5. Physical security. Storage areas are badge-access only. CCTV covers all entrances and aisles, recordings kept for 31 days.
6. Destruction. Records due for destruction are cross-cut shredded on site; a certificate of destruction is issued to the client on request.
