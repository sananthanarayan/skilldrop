# Change brief - locker pickup (Brindlecombe Grocers)

**Launch:** 1 December 2026
**Launch risk owner:** Marguerite Ashdown, Head of Fulfilment
**Data protection officer:** Ravi Dellacourt
**Product owner:** Imogen Sallow

## What changes
Customers of our online grocery can choose to collect from a parcel locker run by LockerLane Ltd
(lockerlane.example). For each locker order we send LockerLane the customer's first name,
surname, mobile number and order number. LockerLane texts the customer a one-time unlock code.

LockerLane uses TextPigeon Inc. (textpigeon.example), based in the United States, to send the
texts. So customer names and mobile numbers go to the US.

Chilled and frozen items stay in a locker for at most 4 hours; after that LockerLane staff move
them to a cold store and the customer must collect from the depot.

Expected volume: about 3,000 locker orders a week in the first quarter.
