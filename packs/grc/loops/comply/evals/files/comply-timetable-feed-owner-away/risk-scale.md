# Risk scale (use this, not another one)

Likelihood (L): 1 Rare, 2 Unlikely, 3 Possible, 4 Likely, 5 Almost certain
Impact (I): 1 Negligible, 2 Minor, 3 Moderate, 4 Major, 5 Severe

Score = L x I.

| Score | Band |
|---|---|
| 1-4 | Low |
| 5-9 | Medium |
| 10-16 | High |
| 20-25 | Very high |

Inherent = before any control. Residual = after controls that have evidence.
A control with no evidence does not reduce a rating.
Any risk whose residual is High or Very high needs its owner's written acceptance.
