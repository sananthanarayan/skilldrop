Subject: Winter timetable - need a decision for the board

All,

The board meets on 19 Nov and wants a recommendation on the winter timetable. Three options on the table:

  A. Keep all 5 daily return crossings (06:10, 07:30, 12:00, 17:15, 21:40).
  B. Cut to 3 return crossings Mon-Fri (07:30, 12:00, 17:15), 5 at weekends.
  C. Drop the 06:10 and the 21:40 every day, keeping 07:30, 12:00 and 17:15, and move the 21:40 to 19:30 so we still run 4.

Since September the 06:10 has averaged 38 passengers. Option C savings as I have them: fuel £84k, crew overtime £51k, maintenance £20k - about £165k a year.

Ailsa
