# Night Shift Warehouse Supervisor — Pellbrook Distribution

Ambient distribution centre, Daventry area · Permanent · £34,500 plus night allowance
Closing date: Sunday 8 November 2026 · jobs.pellbrook.example/night-supervisor

## The role

You'll run the night shift at our ambient distribution centre, leading a team of around 20 pickers, loaders and forklift drivers so that store orders leave on time and accurately.

Shift pattern: 4 nights on, 4 nights off, 22:00–06:00.

## Responsibilities

- Plan and allocate labour across picking, loading and goods-in for the shift
- Hit dispatch and pick-accuracy targets
- Run shift briefings, toolbox talks and return-to-work interviews
- Carry out and record safety checks, and lead incident investigations on shift
- Cover forklift operation when the shift is short

## Requirements

**Essential**
- Current counterbalance and reach truck licences (certificates will be checked at interview)
- At least 2 years supervising a team in a warehouse or distribution centre
- Able to work the 4-on-4-off night pattern
- Experience using a warehouse management system (WMS)

**Desirable**
- First Aid at Work certificate
- IOSH Managing Safely or equivalent
