# Customer Success Manager, Mid-Market — Quindle

Remote (UK) · Full time · Closing date: Friday 6 November 2026
Apply at careers.quindle.example/jobs/csm-mm

## About the role

Quindle makes scheduling and records software for private clinics. You'll own a book of mid-market clinic groups after they go live, keep them renewing, and find room for them to grow with us. You'll report to the Head of Customer Success.

## What you'll do

- Own a portfolio of around 35 mid-market accounts
- Run quarterly business reviews with clinic managers and owners
- Spot at-risk accounts early from usage and health data, and build save plans
- Own renewals and work with Sales on expansion
- Feed customer needs back to Product

## What we're looking for

**Essential**
- 4+ years in a customer-facing role at a software or subscription business, including at least 2 years managing a book of accounts
- Track record on retention or renewals, with numbers to show it
- Experience running business reviews with senior customer stakeholders
- Comfortable working day to day in a CRM

**Nice to have**
- Experience in healthcare or another regulated industry
- Basic SQL or confidence pulling your own data
- Experience onboarding new customers

## What we offer

Salary £42,000–£50,000, 25 days holiday plus bank holidays, home office budget.
