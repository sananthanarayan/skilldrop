# Finance Analyst

**Orrell Leisure Group** · Birmingham · £36,000 – £40,000

Orrell runs 22 gyms across the Midlands. The Finance Analyst reports to the Financial Controller and produces the monthly management pack for site managers.

## What you'll do

- Build the monthly management accounts pack: variance to budget by site, with commentary
- Own the month-end close for payroll and utilities accruals
- Maintain the site-level budget model and reforecast each quarter
- Build dashboards in Power BI for the operations team

## What we need

- Part-qualified ACCA or CIMA, or AAT Level 4 complete
- At least 2 years in a management accounts or analyst role
- Advanced Excel (pivot tables, lookups, modelling)
- Experience preparing variance analysis and budget commentary
- Experience with Power BI or a similar reporting tool

## Nice to have

- Experience in a multi-site business
- Experience with Sage or NetSuite
