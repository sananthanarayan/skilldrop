# Senior Care Coordinator - Community Services

**Tamsworth Valley Health Board** - based at Elsford, with travel across the valley
Reference: HV-2240 | Band 6 | Permanent, full time
Salary: £37,300 - £44,900 | Closing date: 13 November 2026

## About the role

You will coordinate care for adults with complex needs leaving hospital: arranging packages of
care, chairing discharge meetings with families and partner agencies, and keeping the case
record up to date in MedFlow PAS. You will supervise two care coordination assistants.

## Essential

- Current registration with the relevant professional regulator as a nurse, occupational
  therapist, physiotherapist or social worker. Registration will be checked before interview.
- Experience of discharge planning or case management in a health or social care setting
- Experience using a patient administration system
- Excellent organisational skills and the ability to manage a varied caseload
- Full driving licence and access to a car for work

## Desirable

- Experience of supervising staff
- Knowledge of continuing healthcare assessments
