Medical Secretary - Cardiology

Tamsworth Valley Health Board - Elsford Community Hospital

Reference: HV-2198 | Band 4 | Permanent, full time (37.5 hours) | Salary: £26,400 - £28,900
Closing date: 30 October 2026

About the role

You will provide secretarial support to two consultant cardiologists and their team: typing clinic letters from digital dictation, managing their diaries, booking follow-up appointments and acting as a point of contact for patients and GP practices.

Essential

  • Audio typing at 60 words per minute or above
  • Experience as a medical secretary or in a similar healthcare administration role
  • Knowledge of medical terminology
  • Experience of a patient administration system (MedFlow PAS)
  • Level 3 qualification in medical administration, or equivalent experience

Desirable

  • Experience of digital dictation software (we use DictaScribe)
  • Knowledge of RTT pathways
