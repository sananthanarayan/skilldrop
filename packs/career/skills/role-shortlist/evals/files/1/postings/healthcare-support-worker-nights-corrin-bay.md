# Healthcare Support Worker (Nights)

**Tamsworth Valley Health Board** - Corrin Bay Community Hospital, Ward 2
Reference: HV-2253 | Band 3 | Permanent, 30 hours a week, nights only
Salary: £24,100 - £25,600 plus unsocial hours enhancement | Closing date: 4 November 2026

## About the role

Supporting registered nurses to deliver personal care to older inpatients overnight: washing,
dressing, repositioning, taking observations and recording them accurately.

## Essential

- Care Certificate, or willingness to complete it within 12 weeks of starting
- Experience of providing personal care in a hospital, care home or home care setting
- Able to work nights only, including weekends and bank holidays

## Desirable

- Level 2 or 3 qualification in health and social care
