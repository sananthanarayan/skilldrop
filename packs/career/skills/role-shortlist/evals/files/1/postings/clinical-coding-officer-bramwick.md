# Clinical Coding Officer (Trainee)

**Tamsworth Valley Health Board** - Bramwick General Hospital
Reference: HV-2244 | Band 3, rising to Band 4 on completing training | Permanent, full time
Salary: £24,100 - £25,600 | Closing date: 20 November 2026

## About the role

You will read inpatient case notes and translate diagnoses and procedures into ICD-10 and OPCS-4
codes. Full training is provided, including the national clinical coding standards course in your
first year.

## Essential

- Good knowledge of medical terminology and anatomy
- High level of accuracy and attention to detail
- Experience of working with confidential patient records
- Able to work to monthly coding deadlines

## Desirable

- Previous experience of clinical coding using ICD-10 and OPCS-4
- Experience using MedFlow PAS
