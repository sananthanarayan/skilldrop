# Patient Pathway Coordinator

**Tamsworth Valley Health Board** - Bramwick General Hospital
Reference: HV-2231-B | Band 4 | Permanent, full time (37.5 hours, Monday to Friday)
Salary: £26,400 - £28,900 | Closing date: 6 November 2026

## About the role

You will track patients on referral-to-treatment pathways for the surgical specialties, making
sure every patient is booked in date order and no one waits longer than they should. You will
work closely with consultants' secretaries, booking teams and service managers.

## Essential

- At least 2 years' administrative experience in a healthcare setting
- Working knowledge of referral-to-treatment (RTT) rules and pathway tracking
- Experience using a patient administration system (we use MedFlow PAS)
- Level 3 qualification in business or medical administration, or equivalent experience
- Good working knowledge of Excel
- Able to deal calmly and accurately with patients and clinicians by phone

## Desirable

- Experience of waiting list validation
- Experience of preparing weekly breach reports

## Other information

Work is based at Bramwick General Hospital. This post is also advertised at Elsford Community
Hospital under reference HV-2231-E; candidates should apply once and state a preferred site.
