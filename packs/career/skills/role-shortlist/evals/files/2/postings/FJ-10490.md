# Multi-drop Delivery Driver

**Brightside Parcels** - Fenmoor depot
Ref: FJ-10490 | Posted 8 October 2026
Self-employed, paid per drop (around £150 - £175 a day)

## Hours

Days, 9 - 11 hours, five days a week including Saturdays. Loading starts at 07:00.

## The job

Loading your van at the depot and delivering around 120 parcels a day across Fenmoor and the
surrounding villages, using our delivery app.

## What you need

- A full category B driving licence held for at least 2 years, with no more than 6 points
- Registered as self-employed, with your own goods-in-transit insurance
- At least 6 months' multi-drop delivery driving experience

## Nice to have

- Knowledge of the Fenmoor area
