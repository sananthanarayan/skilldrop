# Warehouse Team Leader

**Marrow & Finch Foods** - Fenmoor chilled warehouse
Ref: FJ-10455 | Posted 4 October 2026
Permanent | £31,500 a year plus 15% night shift allowance

## Hours

Nights only: Sunday to Thursday, 22:00 - 06:30. This role is not available on days.

## The job

Leading a team of 12 pickers and drivers on the night shift: allocating work, running the
start-of-shift briefing, checking productivity and accuracy, and making sure every chilled
order leaves on time.

## What you need

- Experience of leading or supervising a warehouse team, formally or as acting team leader
- Reach truck or counterbalance licence
- Experience of cycle counts or stock checks
- Experience in chilled or food distribution

## Nice to have

- Level 2 health and safety qualification
- First aid certificate
