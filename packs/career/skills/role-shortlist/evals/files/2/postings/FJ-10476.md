# Inventory Controller

**Halden Logistics** - Fenmoor Distribution Park
Ref: FJ-10476 | Posted 7 October 2026
Permanent | £27,000 a year

## Hours

Days, Monday to Friday, 08:00 - 16:30.

## The job

Keeping stock accurate across 22,000 pallet locations: planning and running cycle counts,
investigating variances and reporting stock accuracy to the site manager each week.

## What you need

- Experience of cycle counting and investigating stock discrepancies in a warehouse
- Experience of the Korvex warehouse management system, which we use across the site
- Good working knowledge of Excel
- Counterbalance or reach truck licence (you will need to check high locations)

## Nice to have

- Experience of a full annual stock take
