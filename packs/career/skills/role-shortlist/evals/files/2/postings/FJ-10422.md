# Reach Truck Operator

**Halden Logistics** - Fenmoor Distribution Park
Ref: FJ-10422 | Posted 29 September 2026
Temporary to permanent (permanent after 12 weeks) | £13.60 an hour

## Hours

Days, Monday to Friday, 08:00 - 16:30. Occasional paid overtime on Saturdays.

## The job

Putting away and replenishing pallets in our ambient warehouse, racking up to 11 metres,
and carrying out goods-in checks.

## What you need

- A current reach truck licence (refresher within the last 3 years)
- At least 1 year's experience on a reach truck in a warehouse
- Experience using a handheld scanner or warehouse management system
- A good safety record

## Nice to have

- Counterbalance licence
- Experience of cycle counting
