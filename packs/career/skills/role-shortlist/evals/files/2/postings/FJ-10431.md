# VNA Truck Operator

**Brightside Parcels** - Unit 7 Westway, Fenmoor
Ref: FJ-10431 | Posted 1 October 2026
Permanent | £14.90 an hour

## Hours

Days, Monday to Friday, 07:00 - 15:30.

## The job

Operating a man-up very narrow aisle (VNA) truck in our 14-metre high-bay warehouse,
putting away and retrieving pallets in guided aisles.

## What you need

- A current VNA (man-up) truck licence. Applicants without a VNA licence will not be considered;
  we do not provide VNA training for this role.
- At least 2 years' experience driving a VNA truck
- Experience using a warehouse management system

## Nice to have

- Reach truck or counterbalance licence
