Heavy Diesel Technician

Tarnhaven depot · Full time · Ref HDT-0412 · $38–$44 per hour

About the role

Join a workshop team of eight looking after our Tarnhaven fleet of linehaul units and trailers. You'll handle scheduled servicing, breakdown repairs, brake and suspension work, and diagnostics.

Hours

Monday to Friday, 7:00 am to 3:30 pm. Occasional paid overtime on Saturdays, by agreement.

What you'll need

  • A heavy automotive trade certificate (Level 4) or equivalent
  • A current Class 2 licence (required: you'll move units around the yard and road test after repairs)
  • Hands-on experience with air brake systems

Nice to have

  • Experience with laptop-based diagnostic software
  • Trailer and drawbar experience
