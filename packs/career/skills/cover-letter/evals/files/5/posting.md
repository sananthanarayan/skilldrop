# Instructional Designer

**Marlow & Finch Insurance** · Learning and Development · Bristol (hybrid)

You will design and build training for our 1,100 claims and customer service staff: e-learning modules, facilitator guides and short job aids.

## Essential

- Experience designing learning for adults from needs analysis through to evaluation (we use ADDIE)
- Experience building e-learning in Articulate Storyline or Rise
- Experience administering a learning management system
- Able to work with subject matter experts and turn their knowledge into clear learning content
- A portfolio of work samples, shared at application

## Desirable

- Experience in financial services or another regulated industry
- A qualification in learning design (for example CIPD Level 5)
- Video or audio editing

Salary £38,000 – £44,000.
