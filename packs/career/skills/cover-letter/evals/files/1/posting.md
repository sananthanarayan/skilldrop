# Clinical Nurse Educator — Surgical Division

**Northfield General Hospital** · Full time, permanent · Band 6

Northfield General is a 320-bed teaching hospital. The Surgical Division's education team supports 240 nursing staff across five wards.

## The role

You will plan and deliver clinical education for surgical nursing staff: induction for new starters, annual skills updates, and ward-based teaching after incidents and audits.

## You must have

- Current NMC registration (adult)
- A nursing degree
- At least 2 years' experience teaching, precepting or mentoring clinical staff
- At least 3 years' post-registration experience in an acute surgical or medical-surgical setting
- Experience using Epic, including supporting colleagues with it

## It would help if you have

- A master's degree in nursing or education, or to be working towards one
- Basic Life Support instructor certification
- Experience running sessions in a simulation lab
- Experience writing teaching materials or guidelines

## How to apply

Send a CV and a cover letter of no more than one page to the Surgical Education Lead. Closing date 24 October 2026.
