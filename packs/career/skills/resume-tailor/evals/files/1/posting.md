# Staff Engineer, Payments Platform

**Ledgerline** · Stockholm or remote in the EU

Ledgerline processes card and bank payments for 3,000 European merchants.

## What you'll do

- Set technical direction for the payments platform (three teams, about 20 engineers).
- Design services that move money correctly: idempotency, reconciliation, ledgers.
- Raise the bar on reliability; our target is 99.99% availability for the authorisation path.

## What we're looking for

- 8+ years building backend systems, including several in a senior or staff role
- Deep experience with Go or Rust
- Production experience running services on Kubernetes
- Experience leading teams of 8 or more engineers, directly or as a technical lead across teams
- Experience in payments, banking or another regulated financial domain
- Event-driven architecture with Kafka or similar

## Nice to have

- Rust in production
- PCI DSS experience
- Experience with double-entry ledger design
