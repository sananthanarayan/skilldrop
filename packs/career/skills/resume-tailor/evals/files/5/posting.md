Operations Coordinator
Kestrel Freight Solutions - Columbus, OH - Full time

About the job
The Operations Coordinator keeps our cross-dock running to plan: scheduling inbound and outbound loads, coordinating with carriers, and reporting daily performance to the Operations Manager.

Requirements
- Bachelor's degree required
- 3+ years in warehouse or logistics operations
- Experience supervising or coordinating teams of 20 or more
- Advanced Excel (pivot tables, VLOOKUP)
- Experience with SAP Extended Warehouse Management
- Strong safety record and knowledge of OSHA requirements

Preferred
- Lean Six Sigma Green Belt
- Forklift certification
- Bilingual English/Spanish

Pay: $58,000 - $66,000. Day shift, Monday to Friday.
