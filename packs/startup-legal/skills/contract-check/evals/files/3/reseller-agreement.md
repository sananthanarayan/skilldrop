(2) CALDER SYSTEMS DISTRIBUTION LTD, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 08154420), whose registered office is at [                    ] ("Reseller").

## 1. Definitions
1.1 "Commencement Date" means 1 April 2027.
1.2 "Customer" means a public-sector body in the Territory that buys a Subscription from Reseller.
1.3 "Software" means Supplier's Brightmoor Insight planning-analytics platform described in Schedule 1.
1.4 "Subscription" means a right for one Customer to use the Software for twelve (12) months.
1.5 "Price List" means Supplier's list prices set out in Schedule 2, as updated under clause 5.4.
1.6 "Territory" means England and Wales.

## 2. Appointment
2.1 Supplier appoints Reseller as its exclusive reseller of the Software in the Territory, and Reseller accepts the appointment.
2.2 During the Term, Supplier will not sell, license or supply the Software, directly or through any other person, to any public-sector body in the Territory, and will refer every enquiry from such a body to Reseller.
2.3 Reseller has no minimum purchase, sales or marketing obligation under this Agreement.
2.4 Reseller may appoint sub-resellers in the Territory without Supplier's consent.

## 3. Territory
3.1 For the purposes of this Agreement, "Territory" means the United Kingdom and the Republic of Ireland.

## 4. Term and Renewal
4.1 This Agreement starts on the Commencement Date and continues until 31 March 2028 (the "Initial Term").
4.2 At the end of the Initial Term and of each Renewal Term, this Agreement renews automatically for a further twenty-four (24) months (each a "Renewal Term") unless either party gives the other written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days before the end of the then-current term.
4.3 A notice under clause 4.2 must be sent by recorded delivery to the address given in clause 13.2.

## 5. Prices and Payment
5.1 For each Subscription it sells, Reseller will pay Supplier sixty per cent (60%) of the Net Revenue.
5.2 Reseller will pay Supplier within ninety (90) days after the end of the month in which Reseller receives payment from the Customer. Reseller has no obligation to pay Supplier for any Subscription for which the Customer has not paid Reseller in full.
5.3 Reseller will pay Supplier a one-off onboarding fee of fifteen thousand pounds (GBP 50,000) on the Commencement Date.
5.4 Supplier may change the Price List on six (6) months' written notice, but not more than once in any twelve (12) month period.

## 6. Supplier Obligations
6.1 Supplier will provide second-line technical support to Reseller at the service levels set out in Schedule 3.
6.2 Supplier will provide Reseller with sales and marketing materials and reasonable product training.

## 7. Intellectual Property
7.1 Supplier retains all intellectual property rights in the Software.
7.2 Reseller may use Supplier's trade marks in the Territory solely to market the Software.

## 8. Data Protection
8.1 Each party will comply with the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 in performing this Agreement.

## 9. Warranties
9.1 Supplier warrants that the Software will perform materially in accordance with Schedule 1.

## 10. Indemnity
10.1 Supplier will indemnify Reseller against all losses, damages, costs and expenses of any kind arising out of or in connection with the Software or this Agreement.

## 11. Liability
11.1 Nothing in this Agreement limits either party's liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud.
11.2 Reseller's total liability under or in connection with this Agreement will not exceed ten thousand pounds (GBP 10,000).
11.3 Supplier's liability under or in connection with this Agreement is not limited.

## 12. Confidentiality
12.1 Each party will keep the other party's confidential information confidential during the Term and for three (3) years afterwards.

## 13. General
13.1 This Agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales, and the courts of England and Wales have exclusive jurisdiction.
13.2 Notices must be in writing and sent to the registered office of the receiving party stated above.
13.3 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties about its subject matter.
