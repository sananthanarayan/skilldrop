# CONSULTING AGREEMENT

This Consulting Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into on February 30, 2027 (the "Effective Date") by and between Halyard Robotics, Inc., a Delaware corporation with offices at 410 Pier Lane, Port Ardent, Oregon ("Company"), and Quint Industrial Design LLC, an Oregon limited liability company ("Consultant").

## 1. Services
1.1 Consultant will perform the industrial design services described in Exhibit A (the "Services") and deliver the items listed there (the "Deliverables").
1.2 Any change to the Services must be agreed in a written change order signed by both parties.
1.3 Consultant will perform the Services using its own personnel, tools and equipment.

## 2. Fees and Payment
2.1 Company will pay Consultant a fixed fee of Twelve Thousand Dollars ($21,000) for the Services, payable in the installments set out in Exhibit B.
2.2 Consultant will invoice Company for each installment when due. Company will pay each undisputed invoice within thirty (30) days of receipt.
2.3 Company will reimburse reasonable travel expenses approved in advance in writing, up to $[________] per calendar month.

## 3. Term and Termination
3.1 This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues until the Services are complete, unless terminated earlier under this Section 3.
3.2 Consultant may terminate this Agreement for any reason on five (5) days' written notice to Company.
3.3 Company may terminate this Agreement only if Consultant materially breaches it and fails to cure the breach within thirty (30) days of written notice.
3.4 On any termination, Company will pay Consultant the full fee in Section 2.1, whether or not the Services have been performed.
3.5 Sections 5, 6, 9 and 10 survive termination.

## 4. Independent Contractor
4.1 Consultant is an independent contractor. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment, partnership or agency relationship.
4.2 Consultant is solely responsible for its own taxes, insurance and benefits.

## 5. Confidentiality
5.1 "Confidential Information" means non-public information disclosed by one party to the other that is marked confidential or would reasonably be understood to be confidential.
5.2 Each party will use the other party's Confidential Information only to perform this Agreement and will not disclose it to any third party.
5.3 These obligations continue for three (3) years after this Agreement ends.

## 6. Intellectual Property
6.1 Consultant retains all right, title and interest in the Deliverables, including all designs, sketches, CAD files, renderings and prototypes created under this Agreement.
6.2 Upon payment in full, Consultant grants Company a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use the Deliverables for Company's internal business purposes.
6.3 Consultant may display the Deliverables in its portfolio and may license the same or similar designs to third parties.

## 8. Warranties
8.1 Consultant warrants that it will perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike manner.
8.2 EXCEPT AS STATED IN SECTION 8.1, CONSULTANT MAKES NO WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED.

## 9. Limitation of Liability
9.1 Neither party is liable to the other for indirect, incidental or consequential damages.
9.2 Each party's total liability under this Agreement will not exceed the fees paid or payable under this Agreement.
9.3 The limits in this Section 9 do not apply to Company's obligations under Section 10.

## 10. Indemnification
10.1 Company will defend, indemnify and hold harmless Consultant and its members from any claim, loss or expense arising out of Company's use of the Deliverables, including any claim that the Deliverables infringe a third party's intellectual property rights.

## 11. General
11.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Oregon.
11.2 Notices must be in writing and sent to the addresses in Exhibit C.
11.3 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties and may be amended only in a writing signed by both parties.
11.4 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other party's written consent.

## Exhibit A - Services and Deliverables
- Three concept directions for the housing of the Halyard H2 dock-inspection robot
- One refined direction as a CAD surface model (STEP and native files)
- One appearance prototype of the refined direction

- 50% of the fee on delivery of the appearance prototype
