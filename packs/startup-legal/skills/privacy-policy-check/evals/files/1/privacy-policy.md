# TidyHabit Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

TidyHabit ("we") helps you build habits. This policy explains what we collect.

## What we collect

- Your email address and name when you sign up.
- The habits and journal entries you write.
- Payment details, which are handled by our payment processor, Stripe. We never see your full card number.

## How we use it

We use your data to run the app and to send you reminders by email.

## Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties.

## Your choices

You can delete your account at any time from Settings, and we delete your data within 30 days.

Questions? Email privacy@tidyhabit.example.
