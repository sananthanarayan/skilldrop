# Heads of terms — Unit 4, Foundry Yard, Example Town

- Landlord: Foundry Yard Estates Ltd
- Tenant: Brightloop Labs Ltd
- Premises: Unit 4, first floor, 2,100 sq ft
- Term: 5 years from 1 January 2027
- Rent: £36,000 a year exclusive, payable quarterly in advance
- Rent-free: first 3 months
- Rent review: at the end of year 3, to CPI over the preceding three years, compounded, with a cap of 4% a year and a collar of 1% a year; upward only
- Service charge: estimated £4,800 a year, no cap
- Deposit: 6 months' rent
- Tenant break: end of year 3, on 6 months' prior written notice, conditional on all sums due under the lease having been paid and vacant possession being given
- Repair: full repairing; no schedule of condition
- Alienation: no subletting or sharing
- Personal guarantee: from one director for the first 3 years
