# Lease clauses that hurt a small tenant

For each: what to look for, why it hurts, and wording to ask for. Adapt numbers to the deal.

## Break clause

- **Look for:** conditions ("all rent paid", "material compliance with covenants", "vacant
  possession"); notice in a strict form or to a specific address; a penalty; rent-free clawed
  back on breaking.
- **Why it hurts:** courts apply break conditions strictly. A few pounds of unpaid service charge
  or one item left behind can void the break and keep the tenant in to the end.
- **Ask for:** *"The only condition of the break is that the Tenant has paid the principal rent
  due up to the break date."* Delete "material compliance" and "vacant possession" conditions, or
  narrow vacant possession to "free of the Tenant's occupation".

## Rent review

- **Look for:** open-market reviews (unknown increase); upward-only wording; index-linked reviews
  that compound several years at once; no cap.
- **Ask for:** a fixed increase, or index-linked with a cap of 3–4 % a year (compounded) and no
  collar; reviews no more often than every three years.

## Repair, dilapidations and reinstatement

- **Look for:** "full repairing" obligations, especially on an older building; reinstatement of
  all alterations at the end; no schedule of condition.
- **Why it hurts:** the tenant can be billed at the end to put the building in better condition
  than when it moved in.
- **Ask for:** *"The Tenant is not required to put the Premises in better repair than shown in
  the attached Schedule of Condition."* Photograph and attach it. Reinstatement only of
  alterations the tenant made, and only if the landlord asks six months before the end.

## Service charge

- **Look for:** no cap; capital improvements or replacement of plant charged to tenants;
  management fees as a percentage with no limit.
- **Ask for:** a cap at a stated sum rising with CPI; no charges for improvements, replacing
  structure or plant, or remedying defects in the original build.

## Alienation (sharing, subletting, assigning)

- **Look for:** an absolute ban on sharing or subletting; consent needed to share with a group
  company.
- **Ask for:** the right to share with group companies without consent, and to sublet or assign
  with the landlord's consent "not to be unreasonably withheld or delayed".

## Guarantees and deposits

- **Look for:** a personal guarantee from a founder; a deposit of more than six months' rent;
  no terms for its return.
- **Ask for:** no personal guarantee; a deposit of three to six months' rent, held in a
  separate account, returned within 28 days of the end, and released early after two years of
  on-time payment.

## Use, alterations and insurance

- **Look for:** a narrow permitted use that blocks growth (no lab use, no events); consent needed
  for any internal alteration; insurance excluding terrorism or uninsured risks.
- **Ask for:** use "as offices and any other use within the same planning class"; internal
  non-structural alterations without consent; rent suspended if the premises can't be used
  after damage, including by uninsured risks, with a right to terminate if not restored within
  a year.

## When a lawyer earns the fee

- a personal guarantee;
- full repairing terms on a building older than about 20 years;
- a break with conditions the landlord won't narrow;
- a lease that excludes statutory renewal protection where the law gives it;
- a long term (over five years) with open-market reviews.
