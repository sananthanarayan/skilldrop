# Heads of Terms - Subject to Contract

**Property:** Unit 4, Gorse Mill, Canal Street, Halbrook HB3 7QT (ground floor studio, approx. 1,850 sq ft)

**Landlord:** Gorse Mill Estates Limited

**Tenant:** Fernlight Studio Ltd

**Guarantor:** Imogen Marsh (director), personal guarantee of all tenant obligations for the whole term

**Term:** 5 years from and including 1 March 2027, expiring on 1 March 2032

**Rent:**
- Years 1 and 2 (1 March 2027 to 28 February 2029): GBP 24,000 per annum exclusive
- Years 3 to 5 (1 March 2029 to end of term): fixed increase to GBP 26,400 per annum exclusive
- Payable quarterly in advance on the usual quarter days

**Rent-free:** The first 6 months of the term are rent-free. Service charge is payable from the start of the term and is not covered by the rent-free period.

**Service charge:** Tenant to pay a fair proportion of the landlord's costs of the estate. Current budget GBP 3,600 per annum. No cap.

**Repair:** Full repairing and insuring. Tenant to keep the premises in good and substantial repair and condition and yield up in that condition. No schedule of condition.

**Tenant break:** Tenant may terminate the lease at the end of the third year of the term (28 February 2030) by giving the landlord not less than 6 months' prior written notice. The break is conditional on: (i) all rent and other sums due under the lease having been paid in full, and (ii) the tenant giving vacant possession on the break date.

**Use:** Design studio and ancillary offices (Class E).

**Alienation:** No assignment or subletting of part. Assignment of the whole with landlord's consent, not to be unreasonably withheld, subject to an authorised guarantee agreement.

**Security of tenure:** The lease is to be contracted out of the security of tenure provisions of the Landlord and Tenant Act 1954.

**Costs:** Each party to bear its own legal costs.
