# Heads of Agreement (non-binding) - Commercial Lease

**Premises:** Lot 7, 112 Wattle Street, Brunswick VIC 3056 - ground floor laboratory and office, approx. 410 m2

**Lessor:** Wattle Street Holdings Pty Ltd

**Lessee:** Quokka Bio Pty Ltd (ACN 600 000 000)

**Term:** 3 years commencing 1 September 2027 and expiring 31 August 2030

**Option to renew:** One further term of 3 years. The option must be exercised by written notice given not more than 6 months and not less than 3 months before the expiry date.

**Base rent:** A$96,000 per annum plus GST, payable monthly in advance.

**Rent-free (incentive):** 3 months' rent-free from the commencement date. Rent commencement date: 1 November 2027.

**Annual rent reviews:** On each anniversary of the commencement date (1 September 2028 and 1 September 2029) the base rent is reviewed by CPI (All Groups, Melbourne), subject to a minimum increase of 2% and a maximum increase of 5% per review.

**Review on renewal:** At the start of any option term the rent is reviewed to current market rent, provided the rent will not be less than the rent payable immediately before the review.

**Outgoings:** Lessee to pay 100% of the outgoings for the premises (rates, land tax, insurance, building services, common area cleaning and maintenance). Estimate for year 1: A$14,400 per annum plus GST. No cap on annual increases.

**Lessee break:** The lessee may end the lease on 31 August 2029 by giving not less than 6 months' written notice, provided that (a) the lessee is not in breach of any term of the lease at the date of the notice or at the break date, and (b) the lessee pays a break fee equal to 3 months' base rent at the rate payable at the break date.

**Make good:** At the end of the lease the lessee must remove all fit-out and laboratory services and return the premises to base-building condition, at its cost.

**Permitted use:** PC2 laboratory and ancillary office.

**Security:** Bank guarantee equal to 3 months' base rent plus GST.
