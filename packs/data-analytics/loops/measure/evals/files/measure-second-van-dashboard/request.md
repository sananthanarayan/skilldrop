# Dashboard request - Corrowdale Food Network operations board

From: Bea Lindqvist, chair of the operations board
Date: 4 Nov 2026

Each month the board decides which of our five districts the second mobile van visits next month.
We want a dashboard to make that call on. The questions, most important first:

1. Where is demand growing? (households we serve, per district, month on month)
2. Where are people travelling furthest to reach us?
3. Which sites run out of stock before closing?

Could we also have a big counter of "total parcels ever given" at the top? Funders love it.

Owners: Hollis Fenwick (data lead) owns the demand and travel numbers. Esme Tarrant (warehouse
manager) owns the stock numbers. The BI developer starts on 23 November.

The two queries we use today are households_served.sql and stockout_rate.sql. Schema in schema.md.
