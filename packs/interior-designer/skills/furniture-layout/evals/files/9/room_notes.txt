Reading room - Ashby Moor Library - notes for the furniture re-plan
(Rosalind Achebe, branch librarian, Nov 2026)

Room: 10.0 m west to east, 7.2 m south to north, rectangular, no columns.
X and Y in the schedule are to the south-west corner of each piece, from the west
wall and the south wall. Sizes are as the piece sits in the room.

Main door: south wall, opening from 4000 to 4900 mm from the west wall. Opens into the
room, hinged at the west side.
Fire exit: east wall, opening from 5800 to 6700 mm from the south wall. Opens outward.
Window bay: west wall between S1 and S2 (2000 to 3300 mm from the south wall).

S1 and S2 are bolted to the floor and wall and are staying. T5 stays where it is - the
only floor box with power and data for it is under it.

Our standards (from the county's library design guide):
- main routes (main door to the fire exit, and main door to T5) at least 1200 clear
- at least 1000 between shelving ranges
- 900 clear behind the chairs on the long sides of a reading table, to pull the chair
  out (measure from the table edge)
- nothing in the swing of the main door
- a 1500 x 1500 turning space for the wheelchair user at T5, in front of T5 in the
  window bay, so they aren't sitting out in the walkway
