ROOM 214 - accessible king - Harbour Quay Hotel (refurb, furniture install w/c 16 Nov)

Geometry (from the designer's CAD; origin = SW internal corner, x east, y north, mm):
- Overall room 5000 (x) by 5600 (y).
- Entrance lobby: between the built-in wardrobe (x 0-1500) and the bathroom pod (x 2850-5000), from y 0 to y 2400.
  The bedroom is the full width above y 2400.
- Entrance door: south wall, opening x 1600-2500, 900 leaf, hinged at x 2500, opens INTO the room.
- Bathroom door: in the pod's west wall (x 2850), opening y 1200-2050, 850 leaf, hinged at y 2050, opens OUT into the lobby.
- Window: east wall, y 3800-5200.
- The wardrobe and the bathroom pod are built - they are not moving.

BRAND STANDARD - ACCESSIBLE GUEST ROOM (extract, section 4.3)
a) No furniture may overlap other furniture or the fixed joinery, or sit outside the room.
b) Nothing within the swing of any door.
c) Bed: a clear strip at least 1500 wide along one long side of the bed (wheelchair transfer + turning) and at least 800
   along the other long side, measured from the bed edge to the nearest furniture or wall, over the length of the bed
   below the bedside tables. 900 clear at the foot of the bed.
d) 1500 diameter turning circle clear of furniture and walls in front of the bathroom door (the door swing may overlap it).
e) Desk: 750 clear in front of the desk for the chair to pull out.
f) 900 clear route from the entrance door to the bed's 1500 side, to the desk and to the bathroom door.
