# Brief from the Chief Nursing Officer (12 October 2026)

ChartEase drafts nursing notes from the bedside conversation; the nurse reviews and files.

Target: all 1,400 registered nurses live on ChartEase by 1 December 2026.

- Ashby General: 720 nurses
- St Wenna's: 480 nurses
- Larkfield Community: 200 nurses

Commitments you can share with staff:

- ChartEase output will not be used for individual performance management.
- There are no staffing reductions linked to ChartEase.
- Nurses remain accountable for every note and must review each one before it is filed.

Budget to backfill nurses' time while they train is approved.
