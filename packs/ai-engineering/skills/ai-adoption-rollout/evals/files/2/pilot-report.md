# ChartEase pilot report

Pilot: Wards 3 and 7, Ashby General, 1 July - 31 August 2026. 60 nurses.

Headline: ChartEase saved nurses an average of 52 minutes per shift.
(Source: end-of-pilot questionnaire, 11 responses.)

Other findings:

- At the end of the pilot, 34 of the 60 nurses were still using ChartEase on most shifts.
- Ward 7 night shift largely stopped using it after week 2: Wi-Fi dead spots in bays C and D
  meant drafts failed to sync. IT ticket open; no fix date yet.
- 3 notes had medication doses transcribed wrongly. All 3 were caught by the nurse at review.
- Nurses said the best training was a colleague showing them on the ward.
