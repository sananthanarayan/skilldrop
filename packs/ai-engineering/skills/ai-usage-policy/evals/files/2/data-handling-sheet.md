# Larkfield Talent: data handling sheet (v3, January 2025)

Three labels. Everything the firm holds is one of these.

| Label | What it covers | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Public** | Anything already published | Job adverts, the website, published salary guides |
| **Internal** | Day-to-day business information that is not about an identifiable person | Internal process docs, anonymised placement statistics, fee templates |
| **Confidential** | Anything about an identifiable candidate or client contact, and anything a client contract restricts | Candidate CVs, salary and notice details, interview notes, client contracts, call recordings |

## Client-specific terms

**Aldercroft Group** (master services agreement, clause 9.3): candidate data relating to
Aldercroft roles must not be passed to any third-party processor without Aldercroft's prior
written consent.

No other client contract has a clause like this as far as we know. Nobody has checked since
2025.
