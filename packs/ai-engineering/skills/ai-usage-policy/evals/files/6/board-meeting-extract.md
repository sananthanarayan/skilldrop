# Board meeting, 2 October: extract, item 6 (AI tools)

- Murmurly: agreed as a PILOT only, at Northgate and Fernhill clinics, until the contract
  and data processing terms are signed and the pilot is reviewed. Other clinics are not to
  start using it. Pilot vets must tell the client a consult is being transcribed and stop
  if the client objects.
- Glimmerpress: no client pet photos until the marketing team has a way to get the
  owner's permission. No decision yet on how that will work.
- Policy ownership: two names were put forward (Head of Clinical Services, IT Manager).
  Not agreed. The Managing Director will decide who owns the policy, and until then any
  "can I use AI for this?" question not covered by the policy goes to the Managing Director.
- Codewren: decision on buying deferred until the trial ends.
- Ops director to draft a staff policy for the next meeting.
