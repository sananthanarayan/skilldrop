# Legal notes for the AI policy

Bartholomew Nkemelu, Senior Counsel, Ledgerline Payments
22 September 2026

1. **Cardholder data.** Card numbers, expiry dates and security codes must not leave the cardholder data environment. Our PCI DSS scope and our acquiring agreement with Corvane Bank (clause 14.3) both prohibit sending cardholder data to any third party not listed in the agreement. Northgate AI is not listed. A zero-retention addendum does not change this.

2. **Customer personal data.** Under GDPR, customer names, email addresses and transaction history may be processed in AskDesk and CodePilot Enterprise under the signed DPAs. They may not go into any tool without a signed DPA.

3. **Transcribo.** We are still negotiating the sub-processor terms. Do not record customers until the DPA is signed. I cannot give a date.

4. **AI Review Group.** It has not met since June. Idris, who chaired it, moved to the Lisbon office project and was not replaced. Somebody needs to decide who approves exceptions. That is not a legal decision.

5. **Not yet checked.** Whether the last four digits of a card number together with an email address count as cardholder data under our scope. I will confirm with the QSA. Treat as cardholder data until I do.
