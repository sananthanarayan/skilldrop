# Scoring rubric (each criterion scored 0-5; total out of 25)

- C1 Need: evidence of local need.
- C2 Approach: is the plan likely to work?
- C3 Community involvement: are local people shaping and running it?
- C4 Budget value: is the budget realistic and good value?
- C5 Sustainability: will the work continue after the grant ends?

Applications scoring 15 or more go forward to the Grants Committee.
