# Notes: last round and the May trial

Last round: 180 applications received (figure from the annual report). By outcome:

- Met the threshold: 112
- Below the threshold: 38
- Ineligible: 14

May trial: a single AI agent scored 20 past applications. We then asked the same agent to check
its own scores, and it confirmed all 20 (100% agreement). Its scores differed from the officers'
moderated scores by more than 3 points on 7 of the 20.
