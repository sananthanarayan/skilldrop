# Legal advice note (Legal Services to Development Management)

1. Under the Council's scheme of delegation, a decision on a planning application may only be
   issued by a named delegated officer or by the Planning Committee. A decision produced by
   software and issued without an officer's sign-off would be open to legal challenge.
2. Officers may use software tools to help prepare assessments and reports, provided the officer
   reviews and takes responsibility for the content.
3. Requests to applicants for missing validation information may be sent by the admin team on
   officers' behalf, using the approved validation-request templates.
