# IT systems inventory (relevant extract)

- AssetCore (asset management): live since March 2026. Migration status: 62% of water-main and
  pipe records migrated. The remaining 38%, mostly pre-1990 cast-iron mains, are still in legacy
  spreadsheets kept by the network team. Migration due Q2 2027.
- Burst and leak history: recorded in AssetCore from March 2026. Earlier history is in FieldLog,
  the old work-order system, which is due to be switched off. A data export from FieldLog has not
  been tested.
- CustBill v7 (billing): on-premises, no API. The vendor has quoted 9 months to deliver an API
  module.
- Callwise (customer contact CRM): call notes are free text.
- Microsoft 365 tenant in place. No AI tools approved for use.
