# AI ideas: collected from the staff forum and team leads, September 2026

1. **Delivery-date replies (Customer service).** About 600 emails a week ask "where's my kitchen / when is my delivery". That's roughly 40% of the inbox. An assistant could draft the reply from the order status in KitchenTrack and the agent checks and sends. Submitted by the customer service team lead.

2. **Kitchen renders from sketches (Design).** Turn a designer's hand sketch into a photo-style render to show the customer in the showroom. Today renders take a designer about half a day each. Submitted by two designers.

3. **Showroom visit notes (Sales).** Summarise showroom visit notes into a follow-up email. Notes are handwritten in notebooks; there is no CRM. Submitted by a sales manager.

4. **Invoice matching (Finance).** Match supplier invoices to purchase orders automatically. Around 900 invoices a month, matched by hand in the accounts package. Supplier price lists are commercially confidential. Submitted by the finance manager.

5. **Machine breakdown prediction (Manufacturing).** Predict when the edge-banding and CNC machines will fail. Only 3 of 19 machines have sensors fitted; the rest have paper maintenance logs. Submitted by the factory engineer.

6. **Staff handbook Q&A (HR).** A chatbot that answers holiday, sick-pay and shift-swap questions from the staff handbook. HR gets about 40 of these a week. Submitted by HR.

7. **Product descriptions (Marketing).** Write website product descriptions from the spec sheets for the 2027 range (about 120 products). Submitted by marketing.
