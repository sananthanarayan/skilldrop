# Pellmore Kitchens Ltd: background for the AI work

## The company
- Designs, makes and fits fitted kitchens. 240 staff across the factory and the head office/showroom.
- Teams: Manufacturing 130, Operations and logistics 26, Sales 22, Design 18, Customer service 14, Finance 9, Marketing 8, Leadership 6, HR 4, IT 3.

## Sponsor
- Ruth Calloway, Operations Director, sponsors the AI work and signs off the first rollout wave. She wants a first wave live before the January peak.

## Tools
- Office suite with its enterprise AI chat assistant, licensed since June 2026 for 25 seats. The contract says prompts and files are not used to train models and data is held in the UK/EU. Nobody has been given a seat yet; IT holds all 25.
- KitchenTrack, the in-house order system. Holds customer names, delivery addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, order lines, prices paid and delivery slots. No API; IT runs a nightly CSV export of order status.
- Accounts package for finance; no AI features switched on.

## Data
- Customer personal data lives in KitchenTrack and the customer service inbox.
- Supplier price lists are commercially confidential under the supplier agreements.
- Staff HR records are held by HR only.
- The finance manager is also the company's data protection lead (part of the role). There is a data protection policy (2023). There is no AI policy of any kind.

## What people are worried about
- The staff survey (staff-survey.csv) found people already using personal AI accounts at work, including pasting customer emails into chatbots on their phones.
- The customer service team's union rep has asked whether this is about cutting jobs in customer service.
- Finance is worried about supplier pricing leaking.

## Skills and support
- No AI training has been run. IT (3 people) can give two days a month to support a rollout.
