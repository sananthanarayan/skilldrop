# Corrow Lane Accounting LLP: background

## The firm
- 58 people: 6 partners, 14 in the tax team, 21 in accounts and bookkeeping, 9 in payroll, 8 in practice support (admin, IT, marketing).
- Around 1,900 clients: sole traders, small companies and some high-net-worth individuals.

## Sponsor
- Margaret Ilsley, managing partner, sponsors the AI work and will sign off any rollout. She has decided client tax-query replies come first.

## Systems
- Client files live on an on-premises file server and in LedgerDesk, the practice management system. LedgerDesk has no AI features and no export API.
- Email is hosted by our email provider.
- No AI tool is licensed or approved. In August 2026 the IT manager blocked two public chatbot sites after a junior was seen pasting a client's payslip into one.

## Client contracts
- Every client signs our standard engagement letter. Clause 9.2 reads: "We will not disclose your information to any third party, or process it using third-party services other than those listed in Schedule B, without your prior written consent."
- Schedule B lists three services: our email hosting provider, our payroll bureau, and our cloud backup provider. Nothing else.
- Engagement letters are reissued each April.

## Other
- Our professional indemnity insurer's renewal questionnaire, due January 2027, asks whether we use AI on client work and how it is controlled.
- Practice support has one IT manager and no one with AI experience.
- Staff survey (informal, September): 31 of 58 said they would use an AI tool if the firm approved one.
