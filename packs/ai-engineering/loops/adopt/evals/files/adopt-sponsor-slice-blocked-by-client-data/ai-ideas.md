# AI ideas from the October partners' meeting

1. **Client tax-query replies.** Draft replies to client emails asking about their tax position, using the client's file (prior returns, correspondence, working papers). Roughly 350 such emails a month across the tax team; each takes a manager 20 to 40 minutes. Margaret's priority.

2. **Bank statement extraction.** Pull transactions from the PDF bank statements clients send in, ready for bookkeeping. About 1,100 statements a month.

3. **Client call notes.** Record and summarise client calls into the practice management system.

4. **Procedures manual Q&A.** Answer staff questions from the firm's internal procedures manual (onboarding, filing deadlines process, how to raise an invoice). The manual is 140 pages and nobody can find anything in it. No client data in it.

5. **Weekly guidance briefing.** Summarise the week's published tax guidance updates into a one-page staff briefing. Uses only public material.

6. **Newsletter drafts.** Draft the quarterly client newsletter from the partners' bullet points.
