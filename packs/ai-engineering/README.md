# AI engineering

Adopt and build AI systems: readiness assessment, use-case triage, rollout and acceptable-use policy, plus agent loop design, subagent orchestration, spend budgets, agent security review, eval harnesses, and AI-usage reporting.

## Start here: Find out whether a team is ready to adopt AI tools, and what blocks it first

Paste this into Claude Code:

```text
Are we ready for AI? Our support team has 12 people on Zendesk, no AI tools today, and a legal team worried about customer data.
```

- **How to tell it worked:** ai-readiness-assessment scores six dimensions (data, tooling, skills, governance, process, culture) with evidence for each, and ranks the gaps that block adoption first.
- **If nothing happens:** If ai-readiness-assessment does not activate, ask for it by name and check that its folder exists in your skills folder (`~/.claude/skills/` by default).
