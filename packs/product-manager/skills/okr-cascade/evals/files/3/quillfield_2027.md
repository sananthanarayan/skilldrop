# Quillfield Public Libraries — 2027 OKRs and team drafts

## System OKRs for 2027 (approved by the library board)

**O1. Make the library part of more residents' weekly routine.**
- KR1.1 In-person visits across all branches rise from 1.20M (2026) to 1.38M (+15%).
- KR1.2 Active cardholders (card used in the last 12 months) rise from 41,000 to 47,000.
- KR1.3 New card sign-ups among residents aged 16–24 rise 25% (2026: 2,400).

**O2. Grow digital lending without losing print readers.**
- KR2.1 E-book and audiobook loans rise from 310,000 to 400,000.
- KR2.2 Print circulation does not fall below 900,000 (2026: 940,000).

**O3. Close the digital skills gap for older residents.**
- KR3.1 1,500 residents aged 65+ complete a digital skills course (2026: 600).
