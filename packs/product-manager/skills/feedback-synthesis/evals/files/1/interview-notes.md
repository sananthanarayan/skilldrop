# Tallyhouse customer interviews, Q3 2026

Notes by the product team. Text in quotation marks is what the customer said, word for word.
Everything else is the interviewer's summary.

## I-1 — owner, small bookkeeping practice (account A-1042, Growth, UK, customer 3 years)

- Sends about 120 invoices a month for 40 clients.
- The CSV export is the thing she brought up first. Her accountant's software (Ledgerline)
  only imports dates as DD/MM/YYYY, so every month she reformats the export in a spreadsheet.
  "It's an hour of my life every month and I've told you six times."
- Says she has looked at two competitors but "moving 40 clients is a pain, so I haven't".
- Loves the automatic reminders: "The reminders are the reason I'm still here, honestly."

## I-2 — studio manager, design agency (account A-1260, Pro, UK, customer 2 years)

- 58 recurring monthly invoices for retainer clients.
- When the agency raised day rates in July she edited every recurring invoice by hand.
  "When we raised day rates in July I had to open 58 recurring invoices one at a time. Took
  most of a Friday."
- Asked for bulk edit. When asked how often rates change: once or twice a year, plus
  one-off changes when a client renegotiates.
- Reminders: "I don't chase anyone any more."

## I-3 — finance lead, consultancy (account A-1315, Pro, NL, customer 1 year)

- Most clients are in the Netherlands and Belgium and pay by card.
- Since mid-July card payments fail for some clients with an authentication error.
  "Two clients gave up and paid by bank transfer three weeks late."
- Has not found a pattern in which clients fail. Thinks it is "the banks with the app
  approval step", but is not sure.
- Would leave if it isn't fixed by year end: "Getting paid is the whole point of the product."

## I-4 — sole trader, electrician (account A-1402, Starter, US, customer 2 months)

- Mostly uses the iPhone app on site.
- Receipt photo upload crashed once in his first week; worked after an app update.
- Happy so far. On price: "It's fine for now, I only send a few invoices."
- No other problems raised. Interview was short (15 minutes).

## I-5 — co-founder, marketing agency (account A-1188, Growth, IE, customer 2 years)

- Likes the reminders but one went to a client's former finance contact after she had
  updated the contact. "It went to someone who left in March. My client forwarded it to me,
  which was mortifying."
- Price: "Growth went up from 39 to 49 a month and nobody told us why."
- Also mentioned the CSV export puts first and last name in one column (she raised a ticket).
