# Fieldnote sales call notes, September 2026

Written up by the account executives after each call. Text in quotation marks is what the
prospect said; everything else is the rep's summary.

## C-01 — Harrowgate Logistics (1,200 staff), first call, 3 Sep, rep: Dana

- IT director and head of field operations on the call.
- IT director: "We can't roll anything out to 300 engineers without SSO."
- Head of field ops was most interested in route planning across depots.
- Next step: follow-up with procurement.

## C-02 — Pell & Rowe Plumbing (40 staff), 5 Sep, rep: Marcus

- Owner on the call. Wanted to know what happens when a plumber is off sick:
  "Can it move his jobs to someone else without me checking every one?"
- Asked for the per-technician price twice. Rep thinks price is the main hurdle.

## C-03 — Harrowgate Logistics (1,200 staff), follow-up, 11 Sep, rep: Dana

- Procurement and IT director.
- Procurement: "SSO with Entra is a hard requirement for us."
- Deal size discussed: 300 seats. Rep estimate in CRM: £84k a year.
- Status: paused until SSO is on the roadmap with a date.

## C-04 — Brightwater Facilities (650 staff), 12 Sep, rep: Marcus

- Operations director.
- "Our security team will ask about SSO first, then audit logs."
- Liked the dispatcher board. Wants a trial for one region.

## C-05 — Kestrel Electrical (25 staff), 16 Sep, rep: Priya

- Owner. Main question was whether the mobile app works with no signal in basements.
- "Half our jobs are in plant rooms with no reception."
- No questions about security or login.

## C-06 — Norland Housing Services (900 staff), 18 Sep, rep: Dana

- Repairs manager. Interested in scheduling repairs across 14,000 homes.
- Rep note: they'll probably need SSO at some point given their size; it didn't come up on
  the call.
- Asked whether tenants can see a live arrival time.

## C-07 — Quayside HVAC (120 staff), 22 Sep, rep: Priya

- Dispatcher and ops manager.
- Dispatcher: "When someone calls in sick, I spend the morning on the phone re-booking."
- Asked whether Fieldnote warns when a reassigned job clashes with another booking.

## C-08 — Ashdown Cleaning (300 staff), 25 Sep, rep: Marcus

- Finance manager. Compared us with a competitor quoted at a lower per-seat price.
- Rep summary: prospect said pricing per technician feels steep for part-time cleaners.
