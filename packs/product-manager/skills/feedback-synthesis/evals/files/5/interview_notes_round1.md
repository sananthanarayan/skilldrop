# Basilbox: round 1 discovery interviews (my notes)

Recruitment: in-app banner shown to active annual Premium subscribers, 30-minute video call, gift card as thanks. 11 signed up, 6 showed. All six use the app on iPhone or iPad. Notes typed during the call, so quotes are close to verbatim but not perfect.

---

## P1 — lives alone, software tester, cooks 4 nights a week

- Plans on Sunday, shops Monday. Has used Basilbox about 18 months.
- Favourite thing is the aisle-sorted grocery list: "I'm in and out of the shop in fifteen minutes."
- Price: "Honestly it's cheaper than one takeaway a month. I'd pay more."
- Skips most recipes over 40 minutes. Wishes there was a "quick only" filter, but found the time filter when I showed her.
- Imports recipes from two food blogs, no problems.

## P2 — separated, two kids every other week

- "Week A it's me and two hungry kids, week B it's just me." Plans the same way both weeks because the app only lets her set one household size.
- Throws food away on her solo weeks: "I bin a bag of veg every other Sunday and feel awful about it."
- Asked for "a calendar where I can see which weeks are which". When I asked what she'd do with it, she said she'd use it to "make the portions right for whoever's actually at the table".
- Price is fine. Uses the grocery list every week.

## P3 — couple, both work from home

- Heavy recipe importer, "probably 60 percent of my plan is stuff I imported from blogs". Import has always worked for her on iPhone.
- Biggest pain is leftovers: they cook big on Sunday and the plan doesn't know there's lasagne left for Tuesday.
- "The leftovers suggestions are nice, but I have to remember to tell it."
- Price: no concerns. Didn't know there was a free tier.

## P4 — family of five, partner travels Monday to Wednesday

- "Mondays to Wednesdays I'm cooking for four, Thursday on it's five, and at weekends my mum often comes so it's six."
- Edits servings on every recipe by hand: "I do the maths on every single recipe, every week."
- Would like "a calendar view" so she can "see who's home each night".
- Loves the aisle sorting. Said Premium is "worth it, easily".

## P5 — three housemates sharing a plan (iPad on the kitchen counter)

- They take turns cooking; who's home varies week to week with shifts.
- "Half the time we cook for three and it's only two of us, and the third person's portion goes in the fridge and dies there."
- Wants a way for each housemate to mark which nights they're in.
- Splits the subscription three ways, "so it's nothing".

## P6 — retired couple

- Larger text matters to them; found the in-app text size setting useful.
- Cooks from the same 15 or so saved recipes on rotation. Asked what would make them stop using the app, they said "if we lost our recipes. That's years of my wife's favourites in there."
- Hasn't updated the app recently; didn't know about the Sign in with Apple prompt.
- Price: "Very fair."
