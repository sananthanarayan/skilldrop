# ClaimLens — Investment Committee proposal (DRAFT v3)

Harbourline Insurance — Innovation Lab
Sponsor: Priya Okafor (Head of Innovation)

## Summary
ClaimLens reads a claim file (forms, adjuster notes, photo captions, correspondence) and produces
a one-page summary with flagged inconsistencies for the handler. We propose to roll it out as an
internal product, funded by chargeback to the departments that use it.

## Problem
Claims handlers spend a large share of their day reading long files before they can act.
Handlers in the pilot said this is the most tedious part of the job.

## Pilot results (Claims, 25 seats, 6 weeks)
- Weekly active users: week 1 = 22 of 25; week 6 = 11 of 25
- Time saved: average 18 minutes per claim file (self-reported by 3 handlers in a feedback session)
- Satisfaction: "really useful when it works" (feedback session quote)

## Financials
See cost-model.csv.
Running costs are £6,500/month, so **we break even on running costs at 163 seats**
(£6,500 / £40 = 162.5). With Claims alone at 120 seats plus either other department we are
comfortably past break-even.
