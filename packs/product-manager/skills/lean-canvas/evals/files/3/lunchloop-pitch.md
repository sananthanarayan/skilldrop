# LunchLoop — pitch notes (Marco Quill)

**One line:** home cooks in Fernbrook sell freshly made lunches to office workers, ordered by
11am and delivered by 12:30.

**Problem:** Office lunch in Fernbrook is the same three sandwich chains. People are bored and
it's expensive.

**Customers:** Everyone who eats lunch at work. Also the home cooks, who want extra income
from their own kitchens.

**What we've done so far:**
- 14 home cooks signed up after a post in the Fernbrook community Facebook group.
- 3-month pilot in two office buildings. We spent $500 on flyers and lobby tastings and ended
  up with 20 regular customers. The ones who stuck with it ordered about 8 lunches a month
  each and stayed about 6 months on average (some started before the pilot via
  word of mouth).

**Money:**
- A lunch sells for $12. We keep 15% as our fee; the cook gets the rest.
- Delivery costs us $1.10 per order (bike couriers).
- Fixed costs: my salary $7,000 a month plus software and insurance $2,000 a month.

**Market:** There are about 40 office buildings in Fernbrook. I reckon around 1,200 office
workers in total would use something like this.

**Unfair advantage:** Our cooks' food is better than any chain. Once people taste it they
don't go back.

**Ask:** I want to quit my job and build the full app this winter.
