# PawPass — my notes (Dana Okafor, co-founder)

Okay, braindump before the accelerator application is due.

## The idea

PawPass is an app for **mobile dog groomers** — the ones who work out of a converted van
and drive to the customer's house. It sends SMS reminders, takes a card deposit when the
appointment is booked, and rebooks the slot from a waitlist if someone cancels.

## Why

No-shows are killing groomers. My sister runs a grooming salon in town and I spent a
Saturday there and talked to the 6 groomers who work for her. All 6 said no-shows are
"really annoying" and 4 of them said they'd "definitely use an app for it". A report I read
from the Pet Care Trade Association says no-shows cost grooming businesses about 10% of
revenue.

Mobile groomers have it worse, I think, because they've already driven there when the
customer isn't home.

## Who

Early adopters: solo mobile groomers, one van, doing 4–6 dogs a day. They live on their
phones between jobs. Most of them are not big on social media and don't use any booking
software today — paper diary or their phone's calendar.

Later: grooming salons, then vets.

## How we'll get customers

- LinkedIn ads targeting "pet care business owners"
- A booth at PetTech Expo in March (the big pet-industry technology show — mostly chains and
  franchise buyers go, but it's where the industry is)
- Referral credit: one free month for each groomer you refer

## Money

- Price: $29 a month per groomer.
- Our costs per groomer: SMS and card processing come to about $6 a month.
- Fixed costs: hosting $300 a month plus our contract developer at $4,000 a month. Neither of
  us founders takes a salary yet.
- We ran a test LinkedIn campaign last month: spent $1,200 and got 3 paying sign-ups.
- From our 20 beta groomers, people stay about 9 months on average before they quit
  (we looked at a similar app's public numbers and it matched).

## Unfair advantage

Our AI no-show predictor. It looks at past bookings and flags the customers likely to
not show, so the groomer can ask for a bigger deposit. Nobody else has this.

## Goal

300 paying groomers by the end of next year.
