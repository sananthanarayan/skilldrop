# ClubKit — notes (messy, sorry)

## the idea
app for grassroots football clubs. subs collection, match-day availability ("who's playing sat?"),
kit orders, reminders by text. one place instead of 4 whatsapp groups + a spreadsheet + chasing
people for £5 in the car park.

charge the CLUB, not the players. monthly subscription.

## why me
- I've been club secretary at Ferncross Rovers for 6 yrs. I do all of this by hand. it's awful.
- I can build it (I'm a backend dev by day)

## survey (did it in Sept)
posted a google form in the Westmarch Sunday League whatsapp group. 31 responses.
- who answered: 19 players, 8 parents, 4 committee members (secretaries / treasurers)
- "would you use an app like this?" -> 23 of 31 said yes (74%!!)
- "would your club pay £15/month for it?" -> 9 said yes, 14 maybe, 8 no
  - of the 4 committee people: 1 yes, 2 maybe, 1 no
- most common complaint: chasing subs (17 mentions)

## pricing guesses
- £15/month per club. felt right. TeamHuddle (the free app loads of clubs already use for
  availability) doesn't do subs properly, so I think clubs will pay for that bit.
- maybe £120/year option later

## costs per club per month (my estimates)
- hosting: £1.20
- SMS reminders: £2.30 (assumes ~50 texts/club/month)
- payment processing: 2% of the subscription

## ads test (Oct)
- spent £300 on facebook ads targeting club admins in Westmarch county
- 6 clubs signed up for the free 30-day trial
- 2 of them converted to paid at £15/month (as of last week)

## churn
no idea really. guessing 5% a month? that's what I read is normal for small SaaS.
NB most clubs basically shut down June-August (no fixtures)

## the dream
- there are ~1,200 grassroots clubs registered with Westmarch County FA
- 400 clubs x £15 = £6,000/month. I need about £3,500/month take-home to quit my job.
- so 400 clubs and I'm out!!
