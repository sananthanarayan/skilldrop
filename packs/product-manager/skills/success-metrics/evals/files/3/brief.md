# Donor return reminders — feature brief, Kestrel Blood Service

## The feature
After a first-time donor gives blood, we send SMS reminders with a link to book their next donation. Phone booking stays available.

## Goal (from the donor programmes manager)
"Our repeat donation rate is 35%. Target: 50% of first-time donors donate again within 30 days of their first donation."

## Data export (donor database, last full year)
- First-time donors: 15,200
- Of those, donated again within 12 months: 4,410

## Facts and constraints
- Whole-blood donors must wait at least 12 weeks between donations.
- The SMS vendor reports delivery only. It does not report link clicks.
- The booking system records the channel ("web" vs "phone") only for web bookings; phone bookings have no channel field.
- Launch date: March 1. The communications team's national TV campaign "Give Again" also starts on March 1.
- Donors can reply STOP to opt out of SMS; the vendor logs opt-outs.
