# Calder City Rent Relief — research notes for the tenant journey map

## Programme manager's view
"Our biggest problem is document upload. We lose a huge share of applicants there. Overall only 1,180 of 4,800 applicants were paid — a 1 in 3 success rate."

## Application funnel (last 6 months)
| Step | Applicants reaching it |
|---|---|
| Started application | 4,800 |
| Finished eligibility check | 4,350 |
| Uploaded all documents | 3,100 |
| Landlord confirmation received | 1,500 |
| Approved | 1,320 |
| Paid to landlord | 1,180 |

About 40% of applications were started in Spanish.

## Tenant interviews (6 tenants, all interviewed in English)
- T1: "The eligibility questions were fine. Took ten minutes."
- T2: "I uploaded my pay stubs on my phone. Two got rejected as blurry, so I did them again at the library."
- T3: "My landlord never answered the email from the city. I called him three times. I had no idea what was happening for weeks."
- T4: "I didn't know if my application was stuck or just slow. Nobody told me anything."
- T5: "I was scared we'd be evicted before the money came. The notice said 14 days."
- T6: "When it got approved I cried. But then it was another two weeks before the landlord got paid and he was still angry."

## Funding rules
- The landlord confirmation step is required by the state funding rules and cannot be removed.

Map the tenant's journey from finding out about the programme to the rent being paid, and tell us where to focus. Keep it to two pages.
