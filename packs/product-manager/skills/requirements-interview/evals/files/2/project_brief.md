# Faster discharge — project brief, St Aldric's General Hospital

Sponsor: Chief Financial Officer
Prepared by: Patient Flow programme lead

## Problem as the sponsor sees it
"Discharges are slow because doctors write discharge summaries late. If we fix the summaries, beds free up before noon."

## What the data says (discharge audit, Ward 7, last 3 months)
- Median wait from "medically fit for discharge" to leaving the ward: 5.4 hours.
- Median wait for the discharge summary to be signed: 1.2 hours.
- Median wait for take-home medicines from pharmacy: 3.1 hours.
- Median wait for patient transport (for patients who need it): 1.6 hours.
- Discharges before noon: 14% of 1,200 monthly discharges, i.e. 280 patients.

## Constraints
- The CFO has confirmed there is no budget for new staff this year. Any change must use existing staff.
- We need the requirements by the end of next month, so interviews must happen in the next two weeks.
- Each interview slot is 20 minutes.
- Interviewing patients directly needs ethics approval, which takes about six weeks. The Patient Liaison Officer can share themes from discharge-related complaints.

## Who we will interview
1. The CFO (sponsor)
2. Ward 7 nurse manager
3. Pharmacy lead
4. Patient transport supervisor
