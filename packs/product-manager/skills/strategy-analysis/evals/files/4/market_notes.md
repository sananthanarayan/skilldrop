# Corrin Coachlines — airport shuttle market: notes for the board

Question from the CEO: "Should we launch an airport shuttle between the city and Wrenmoor Field Airport? And what market share will we win in year 1?"

## Demand
- Consultant's deck (Halvard Advisory): "Passenger traffic at Wrenmoor Field is growing 12% a year."
- Airport authority annual reports, total passengers: 2023: 4.1M, 2024: 4.3M, 2025: 4.4M.
- Consultant's market size: "1.2M shuttle-eligible trips a year x $28 average fare = $46M market."

## Competition
- Bluewing Shuttle (incumbent): runs curbside shuttles every 20 minutes, fare $32.
- Ride-hailing: typical city-to-airport fare about $45.
- City rail link: none; a rail extension has been discussed for years with no funding.

## Access
- The airport's ground-transport concession gives Bluewing Shuttle exclusive curbside pickup and drop-off rights until June 2029.
- Off-airport options: a park-and-ride lot 1.5 km from the terminal, linked by the airport's free terminal bus.

## Our position
- 38 coaches, mostly used for school runs and weekend charters; about 9 coaches sit idle between 9:00 and 15:00 on weekdays.
- Strong safety record; brand known for school transport, not for travellers.
