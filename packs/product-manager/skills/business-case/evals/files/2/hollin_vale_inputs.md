# Hollin Vale Food Network — inputs for the cold-chain decision

Compiled by the operations lead for the November 20 board meeting.

## Background

We collect surplus produce from 14 farms and 6 supermarkets and distribute it through 22 partner pantries. Today volunteers collect in their own cars. Produce can sit unrefrigerated for up to 5 hours between pickup and our warehouse.

## Spoilage

- For our annual report we value donated produce at $1.90 per kg.

## Options

**A. Buy a refrigerated 3.5-tonne van.** Dealer quote: $68,000. Insurance, maintenance and fuel estimated at $9,500 a year. It needs a paid driver three days a week: $24,000 a year. Expected life 8 years.

**B. Lease the same van.** Dealer quote: "$1,850 per month, i.e. $19,200 per year", 3-year term, maintenance included. Insurance and fuel estimated at $6,000 a year. Same paid driver as option A.

**C. Keep volunteer drivers as today.** No new cost. The volunteer coordinator reports that 3 of our 11 regular drivers are leaving by spring.

## Funding

- Mereford Community Trust grant: up to $70,000, for capital purchases only (vehicles or equipment the network will own). It cannot pay for leases, salaries, or running costs. Application deadline: December 1.
- The treasurer says the operating budget has $20,000 a year of headroom for new recurring costs.

"Make the case, and tell the board how many more families we'll feed."
