# Research

Take a question from vague to answered: plan the research around the decision it serves, synthesise the sources with every claim cited, and rank rival explanations by the evidence against them.

## Start here: Turn a vague research request into a plan you can finish

Paste this into Claude Code:

```text
I need to research whether our 14-person support team should move to a four-day week. Our COO decides by 1 November. I have about 20 hours and can pull ticket data from Zendesk.
```

- **Before you start:** A question you need answered and the decision it feeds; for synthesis, the text of your sources, not just titles
- **How to tell it worked:** research-plan writes a plan that opens with the decision, the decider and the date, then gives three to six sub-questions, each with a finding that would change the decision, a method, search strings, and a stopping rule with a budget cap.
- **If nothing happens:** If you get a plan built around a decision you didn't give, say who decides what and by when, and run it again. If research-plan does not activate, ask for it by name ("use research-plan").
