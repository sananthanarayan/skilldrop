# Ploverly mobile 4.2 — release notes

Released Tuesday 6 October 2026 to 100% of iOS and Android users (no staged rollout).

- New bottom navigation bar replaces the side menu
- "Invoices" and "Payments" merged into one "Money" tab
- "Download invoice PDF" moved from the invoice screen into the "..." menu
- Dark mode
- Bug fixes

The web app was not changed in this release.
