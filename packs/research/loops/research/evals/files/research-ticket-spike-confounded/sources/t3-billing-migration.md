# Billing platform migration — completion note

Author: Finance systems · 2026-10-07

The move from the old billing system to the new one finished over the weekend of 3–4 October 2026. From 5 October, all customers' subscription invoices (web and app customers alike) are generated by the new system.

Customer-visible changes:
- Invoice numbers changed format (PLV-000123 became 2026-10-000123)
- Subscription invoices are now emailed from billing@ploverly.example instead of accounts@ploverly.example
- A known issue: some customers saw a duplicate "pending" card authorisation on 5–6 October. The second authorisation drops off on its own within 7 days and was never charged. We do not yet know how many customers were affected.

Finance has not reviewed support tickets about billing since the migration.
