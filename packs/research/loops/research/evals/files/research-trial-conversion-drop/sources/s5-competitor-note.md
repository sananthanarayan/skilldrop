# Competitor watch: Vantor free tier

Author: Growth team · 2026-09-12

Vantor announced a free tier for sole traders on their blog on 10 September 2026 (blog.vantor.example/free-for-sole-traders). Limits: one bank account, 20 invoices a month, no VAT returns.

Our Starter plan includes VAT returns, which the Vantor free tier does not.

We have no data yet on how many of our trial users also tried Vantor.
