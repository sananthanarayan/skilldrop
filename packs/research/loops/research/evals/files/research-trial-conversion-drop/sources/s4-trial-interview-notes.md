# Calls with September trial users who did not convert

Interviewer: Femi Adebayo (Growth) · calls 5–9 October 2026 · 5 calls, 20 minutes each
Recruited from trial users whose trial started 7–18 September and who did not pay. 31 emailed, 5 agreed.

**P1 — sole trader, decorator**
"I signed up and then didn't really know where to start. I was waiting for some kind of guide." Did not recall receiving any emails from Tallyfold. Found them in spam during the call.

**P2 — small bakery, two staff**
Got stuck connecting the bank feed. "I assumed there'd be a walkthrough." No recollection of onboarding emails.

**P3 — freelance designer**
Said the price was too high compared with "Vantor, which is free now". Had looked at the pricing page before signing up.

**P4 — dog groomer**
"Never got the setup email, I don't think." Gave up after the first day.

**P5 — consultant**
Busy month, didn't have time to try it properly. Might come back in January.

Interviewer's note: small sample, self-selected. Three of five mention not getting or not finding the setup guidance.
