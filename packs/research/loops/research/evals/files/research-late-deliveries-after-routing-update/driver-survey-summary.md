# Driver survey - summary (run 23-25 Sep 2026, 62 responses across all four depots)

- 71% said that since early September their routes send them back across areas they have
  already covered, "zig-zagging" between delivery windows.
- Eastmoor drivers (no new starters) gave the same complaint at about the same rate as Northgate.
- 18% mentioned staffing or being short-handed.
- 9% mentioned the September storms (all from Saltbridge or Kellow).

Free-text example: "The van goes past the same estate three times a day now."
