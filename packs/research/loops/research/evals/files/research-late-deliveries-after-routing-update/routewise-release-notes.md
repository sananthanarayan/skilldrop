# RouteWise 4.2 release notes (vendor: RouteWise Systems, routewise.example)

Deployed to all Brackenridge depots overnight 1-2 September 2026.

## What changed
- New time-window clustering: stops are grouped by customer delivery window first and by
  geography second.
- Faster re-optimisation when a stop is added mid-route.

## Rollback
Customers may request a rollback to 4.1 within 60 days of release. Contact your account manager.
