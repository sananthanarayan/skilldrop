# LumaRead services agreement - extract (schedule 3, service levels)

3.1 LumaRead will make the service available 99.5% of each calendar month, as measured on its
status page (status.lumaread.example).

3.2 If monthly availability falls below 99.5%, the Library may claim a service credit of 5% of
that month's fee. Claims must be made within 60 days of the end of the month.

---
