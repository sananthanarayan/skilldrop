# Staff survey on office days: summary

Compiled by Odette Marsh, studio manager, 12 March 2026.
Sent to all 45 staff. 38 responses. Anonymous.

## Q1. How many days a week would you choose to be in the studio?

| Answer | Responses |
|---|---|
| 2 or fewer | 27 |
| 3 | 9 |
| 4 or 5 | 4 |

## Q2. "The 3-day rule helps me do my job well."

| Answer | Responses |
|---|---|
| Agree | 11 |
| Neutral | 9 |
| Disagree | 18 |

## Q3. Main reason you come in (pick one)

| Answer | Responses |
|---|---|
| Design reviews and crits | 19 |
| Mentoring / being mentored | 8 |
| Client meetings | 7 |
| Home is not a good place to work | 4 |

## Free text

- 7 respondents identified themselves as Part 1 or Part 2 assistants. 6 of those 7 wrote
  that they learn more on days when senior staff are in.
- "Tuesdays are great. Fridays I sit on my own with my headphones on."
- "I'd come in more if I knew my team would be there."
- "Three days is fine, it's being told which three I mind."

## Limits

No breakdown by team or seniority beyond the assistants who identified themselves.
