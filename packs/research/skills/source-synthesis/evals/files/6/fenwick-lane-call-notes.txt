Call with Tom Ashdown, practice manager, Fenwick Lane Vets (4 vets, edge of town, about 20 min from us)
My notes, 9 Oct 2026

- They started Saturday mornings 9-1 about eleven months ago.
- First six months were slow: "eight or nine appointments a session, some weeks fewer".
- Picked up after that. By month ten they were at about twelve a session. Now month eleven, similar.
- Staffing cost per Saturday about £740. That includes paying a locum vet at a premium, because their own vets would not cover weekends at first.
- He thinks it will pay for itself in year two but "not this year, honestly".
- Most Saturday bookings are vaccinations and minor stuff, not surgery.
- He said he hasn't looked at whether Saturday clients are new or people who'd have come in on a weekday anyway.
