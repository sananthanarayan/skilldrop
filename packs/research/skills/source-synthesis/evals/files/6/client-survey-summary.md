# Brackenmoor Veterinary Practice: client survey on opening hours

Run by reception, March 2026. Emailed to 2,950 active clients; 412 replied (14%).

**Question: "If we offered appointments on Saturday mornings (9am to 1pm), would you use them?"**

| Answer | Share of replies |
|---|---|
| Definitely | 38% |
| Probably | 27% |
| Unlikely or no | 35% |

Free-text comments mostly mentioned work hours ("I can never get there before 6") and vaccination boosters.

Note from reception: we did not ask whether people would use a Saturday slot instead of a weekday visit or as well as one, or how often they would come. The survey went to clients with an email address on file, so older clients are probably under-represented.
