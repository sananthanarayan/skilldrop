# Behaviour logs: pilot schools

Extract by the trust data team, 9 July 2026. Counts of logged behaviour incidents per term.

## Ashcombe School (1,620 pupils)

| Year | Autumn | Spring | Summer | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2024-25 (before pilot) | 150 | 138 | 124 | 412 |
| 2025-26 (pilot year) | 141 | 92 | 85 | 318 |

Note: on 5 January 2026 Ashcombe moved from the old paper-and-spreadsheet log to ClassLedger. Under ClassLedger, "low-level disruption" is recorded as a classroom note and is no longer logged as an incident. In 2024-25, low-level disruption accounted for 96 of Ashcombe's 412 incidents.
