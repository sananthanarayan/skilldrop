# Teacher focus group: notes and quotes

Held 12 May 2026, Ashcombe School library, 50 minutes. Six teachers, all from Ashcombe. Facilitated by Hollis Tran. A session at Fenwick Park was planned for June but did not take place.

[04:10] Maths teacher: "I used to have one argument a week about a phone under the desk. I've had maybe two all year."

[09:30] Head of Year 9: "Form time is a mess now. Pouching takes ten minutes every morning. That's ten minutes of registration and notices gone."

[14:45] English teacher: "Some of them bring an old dead phone to put in the pouch and keep the real one. I don't know how many. More in Year 10 and 11."

[21:00] Science teacher: "I miss them for practicals. We used phone timers and the slow-motion camera. I've had to book the tablet trolley and there's one trolley for the department."

[27:20] PE teacher: "Lunchtime is louder. They actually talk to each other. I'd call that a good thing but the duty staff might not."

[33:05] Head of Year 9: "The gate at ten past three is the flashpoint. One unlocking station per year group, two hundred and seventy kids. We've had pushing."

[41:15] Maths teacher: "Would I go back? No. But I wouldn't say behaviour is transformed. Lessons are calmer. Corridors are the same."

[46:00] English teacher: "Nobody has asked the pupils."
