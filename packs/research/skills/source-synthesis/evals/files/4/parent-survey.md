# Parent survey: summary

Online survey, open 1–19 June 2026. Sent to all 3,050 households with a child at Ashcombe or Fenwick Park. One response per household.

Responses received: 1,140 (Ashcombe 790, Fenwick Park 350)

## Q1. Should the pouch scheme continue next year?
| Answer | Share of respondents |
|---|---|
| Yes | 58% |
| No | 24% |
| Not sure | 18% |

Results were not broken down by school.

## Q2. What, if anything, worries you about the scheme? (tick all that apply)
| Concern | Share of respondents |
|---|---|
| Not being able to reach my child in an emergency | 41% |
| Cost of replacing a lost pouch | 19% |
| My child needs their phone for a medical reason | 6% |
| Nothing worries me | 37% |

## Q3. Has your child's behaviour at home around their phone changed?
