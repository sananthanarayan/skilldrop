# Peer mentoring and first-year retention: a mixed-methods study

## Abstract

We studied whether pairing first-year students with trained second-year mentors improves retention into the second year. Across three cohorts (n = 1,240), mentored students were retained at 91% against 84% for a matched comparison group. Interviews suggest the effect works through belonging rather than academic help.

## 1. Introduction

Retention in the first year predicts degree completion (Tinto, 1993; Kuh et al., 2008). Peer mentoring is widely used but rarely evaluated with a comparison group (Crisp & Cruz, 2009). Walton and Cohen (2011) showed that a brief belonging intervention raised grades, and Yeager (2016) extended the finding to larger samples.
