# Oakmere Libraries - Home Library Service relaunch

Doc: https://wiki.example.test/libraries/home-library-service-relaunch
Owner: Rhiannon Price, Community Services Manager
Status: approved by the libraries leadership team, 5 Oct 2026

## 1. Background

The Home Library Service brings books to members who cannot get to a branch because of age, illness or disability. Volunteer drivers deliver once a month. 180 housebound members use the service today, and referrals have been waiting about six weeks for a first delivery. We want to grow the service by 50% by the end of March 2027.

## 2. Goals

- **G1.** Serve 250 housebound members by 31 March 2027.
- **G2.** Cut the wait from referral to first delivery from six weeks to two weeks.

## 3. Non-goals

- Delivering to members who are not housebound.
- Changing the mobile library van routes.

## 4. Requirements

| ID | Requirement | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | A member, or someone caring for them, can request books by phone, and the request is taken by a named member of staff. | Must |
| R2 | Health and social care workers can refer a member using an online referral form. | Must |
| R3 | Every volunteer driver has a background check and completes safeguarding training before their first delivery. | Must |
| R4 | At least 400 large-print and audiobook items are reserved for Home Library Service members. | Should |
| R5 | Members get a reminder phone call two days before each delivery. | Should |
| R6 | Deliveries every two weeks instead of monthly. | Could |
| R7 | Use a paid courier for areas with no volunteer drivers. | Won't (this phase) |

## 5. Volunteers

We need 12 more volunteer drivers to meet G1 and G2.

| ID | Requirement | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| R8 | New volunteers must pass a background check and finish safeguarding training before they go out on any delivery. | Must |
| R9 | Each volunteer is matched to no more than 8 members, so visits stay personal. | Should |

## 6. Open questions

- **Q1.** Do we deliver to people living in care homes, or do care homes stay on the existing bulk-loan scheme? Rhiannon to decide by 30 Oct 2026.
