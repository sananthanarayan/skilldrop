# ADR-0001: Record architecture decisions

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2023-09-04
- **Decider:** Rosalind Achebe-Marr

## Context

Decisions were made in meetings and lost. New engineers could not tell why things were built the way they were.

## Decision

We record each significant architecture decision as a short ADR in `adr/`, using the sections Context, Decision, Consequences. Each ADR names its decider and date. Risks the decider accepted are listed under Consequences, so nobody has to rediscover them.

## Consequences

- The `adr/` folder is the history of why.
- An ADR records a decision; it is not where the decision is argued.
