# Customer commitments that touch engineering

Kept by the commercial team so engineering can see what we have promised. Wording copied from the signed contracts.

## All tenants (standard terms, clause 9.1)

> Tillowmere will keep Customer Data logically separated from the data of other customers.

## Mosel Steuerberatung (signed 2025-04-02, clause 7.2)

> All Customer Data, including backups and replicas, shall be stored and processed exclusively within the European Union.

## Norderhof Treuhand (signed 2025-07-15, clause 6.4)

> Customer Data, including backup copies, shall not be stored outside the European Union at any time.

## Fenrow Lowe (signed 2024-01-20, clause 11)

> Tillowmere will restore Customer Data to a point in time within the previous 7 days within one business day of a written request.
