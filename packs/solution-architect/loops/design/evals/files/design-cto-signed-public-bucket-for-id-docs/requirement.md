# Requirement: tenants upload proof of identity

Ratified 2026-10-30 by the Larchmont Lettings product group.

Prospective tenants upload a photo of a passport or driving licence and a
recent utility bill during their application. Letting agents view them to
complete right-to-rent checks.

- About 1,200 applications a week; up to 3 files each, up to 10 MB per file.
- Agents must be able to view a tenant's documents for 30 days after upload.
- After 30 days the documents must be deleted (legal retention limit).
- Only the agent handling the application, and the tenant, may see the files.
