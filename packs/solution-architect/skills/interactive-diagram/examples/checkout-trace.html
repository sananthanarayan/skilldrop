Where a slow checkout spent its time

Why did this checkout take 1.8 seconds?

Two thirds of it was the Stripe call, and most of that was waiting on Stripe, not the TLS handshake. A cache miss on prices cost another 350 ms before payment even started.

What it shows
  • The whole of POST /checkout took 1.8 s.
  • What the whole waited on most: stripe.PaymentIntent.create, 1.1 s of its own work (60%); select price from products (cache miss), 346 ms of its own work (19%); insert into orders, 165 ms of its own work (9%).
  • POST /checkout takes 1.8 s but only 27 ms of it is its own; the rest is waiting on what it calls.
  • price cart takes 380 ms but only 15 ms of it is its own; the rest is waiting on what it calls.
