# Coldharbour Orchards Co-operative: Brand Guidelines (2024)

## Who we are
A co-operative of 38 family orchards in the Coldharbour valley, pressing cider and apple juice since 1987.

## Colours
- **Orchard Green** #2E5E3A: primary. Headings, logo, buttons.
- **Russet** #A0522D: secondary. Labels and highlights.
- **Blossom Yellow** #F2C94C: accent. Use Blossom Yellow for body text on white backgrounds in leaflets so they feel sunny.
- **Cream** #FBF6EA: page backgrounds.

## Fonts
- Headings: Fenwick Serif
- Body: Larkspur Sans

## Logo
- `logo.svg` on light backgrounds.
- `logo-white.svg` on dark backgrounds and photos.
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the apple.

## Voice
Warm, plain-spoken, proud of the land. Short sentences. We never say "artisanal" or "craft". We say "our members' orchards", not "our farms".

## Imagery
Real members' orchards and people only. No stock photos of apples.
