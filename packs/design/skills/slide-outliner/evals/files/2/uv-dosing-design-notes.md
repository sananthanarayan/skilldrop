# Millstone Treatment Works: UV disinfection upgrade
## Design notes for the Kestrel Bay Water steering committee

Slot: 20 minutes at the 4 December steering committee, including questions. No more than 10 slides.

## Summary
Replace the chlorine contact tank stage at Millstone with UV disinfection. Total capex £4.2m. Expected completion autumn 2028.

## Why
- Two disinfection by-product (THM) exceedances at the Hallow Hill sample point in 2025.
- Chlorine gas deliveries through Millstone village: 26 a year, with repeated parish council complaints.
- Contact tank concrete rated "poor" in the 2024 condition survey.

## What changes
- Two UV reactor trains (one duty, one standby) after filtration.
- Chlorine dosing is kept, at a lower dose, to hold a residual in the distribution network. UV leaves no residual.
- Switch from chlorine gas to sodium hypochlorite made on site, which ends gas deliveries.

## Cost (estimate rev C)

| Item | £m |
|---|---:|
| UV reactors and controls | 1.9 |
| Civils and building | 1.1 |
| Hypochlorite system | 0.4 |
| Electrical upgrade | 0.4 |

## Approvals
- Drinking water regulator: application submitted 30 September 2026; decision expected Q1 2027.
- Planning: not needed (permitted development), confirmed by the council.

## Risks
- UV reactor lead time is currently 40 weeks.
- UV adds about 85 kW of load; the site supply upgrade needs the network operator's agreement, which has not yet been requested.
- Lamp breakage: a mercury handling procedure is needed before commissioning.

## Ask
Steering committee to approve £4.2m capex and release £0.6m now for detailed design.
