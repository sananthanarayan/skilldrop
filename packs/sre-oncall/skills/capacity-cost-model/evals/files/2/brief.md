# Smart metering platform: cost estimate request

From: Programme Manager, Smart Metering Programme, Brackwater Water
To: Platform team

We need a cloud cost estimate for the meter data platform before the finance committee meets on 4 November.

What we know:

- We are replacing 120,000 household water meters with smart meters.
- Each meter sends a reading every hour, so we're looking at about 1 million readings a day.
- Readings land in the time-series database and a daily file of per-meter totals goes to our on-premises billing system.
- The regulator requires us to keep reading-level data for 7 years.
- Finance has pencilled in $1,500 a month for running costs.

What the committee wants:

1. Monthly running cost in year 1 and year 2.
2. What happens if the three neighbouring utilities join our platform. They are each roughly our size, so call it 3x.
3. The payback period in months, so they can compare it with the old meter-reading contract.

Please keep it to two pages at most. The committee will not read more than that.
